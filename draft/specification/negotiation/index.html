    Negotiation¶

    Version 2026-10-18

    This page is part of BACP version 2026-10-18. Rules marked Proposed depend on an open question and may change in a later dated release.

    The negotiation module lets an agent bargain, request quotations and bid on behalf of a principal, the way trade happens in India today: mol-bhav at a kirana or a weaver's shop, DM bargaining on social commerce, RFQ → quotation → PO in B2B procurement, and reverse auctions on government platforms. It sits before checkout. A negotiation ends in an agreed term sheet that the seller then binds in its signed on_init quote, and only that signed quote enters the checkout session and the payment flow.

    RFQs and auctions build on this page and are specified in RFQ and Auctions. Which modes, payment handlers and validators apply to each kind of trade is set by the trade profiles.

    Principles¶

    1. Only structured, signed and expiring messages bind. Chat, voice, counterparty free text and LLM rationale are advisory. They carry binding: false, are logged as hashes, and never change a term sheet.
    2. The model proposes and a deterministic engine decides. A guard outside the LLM evaluates every outgoing firm counter-offer, acceptance, bid and award, and every incoming offer, against the principal's negotiation mandate. The model sees only a verdict.
    3. The reservation price never leaves the principal's side. The seller verifies only a signed within_bounds attestation. No message, error, webhook or log line sent to anyone else carries the reservation price or the ceiling.
    4. Indian law is enforced as validators, not left as guidance. MRP ceilings, the MSMED credit cap, the dark-pattern rules of the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA), cash limits and bid-rigging screens are checks that block, escalate or flag.
    5. Contract-law alignment. Offer, revocation, lapse and acceptance follow the Indian Contract Act, 1872, ss.4–7 (see Indian Contract Act timing).
    6. Silence is never acceptance. A negotiation that receives no reply ends TERMINATED at its deadline, never AGREED.

    Negotiation modes¶

    Every session declares its mode up front. The enum is negotiation-session.json#/$defs/Mode.

    mode How trade does it today Mechanics in BACP Acceptance or award
    fixed Catalogue checkout, MRP goods One firm offer (the on_select quote). The buyer may only accept, reject or let it lapse. Buyer accept
    posted_dynamic Rail flexi-fare, airline buckets, Tatkal A firm offer with short validity and a quota or class. A price change after expiry is a new offer, never a silent charge. Buyer accept within validity
    alternating Mol-bhav, DM bargaining, car dealers, B2B price rounds Alternating firm or indicative offers. One live offer at a time, max_rounds, absolute deadline. Either side accepts the live offer
    rfq_sealed B2B RFQ, GeM e-bid, sealed tenders Structured RFQ to N sellers, sealed commit–reveal quotations with a deposit, landed-cost comparison Award after response_by (RFQ)
    rfq_iterated Shortlist and "best and final" rfq_sealed, then alternating rounds with a shortlist. Each shortlisted seller is its own bilateral session under the parent rfq_id. As above
    english Mandi open outcry, eNAM, disposal auctions Ascending price-only bids on a neutral auctioneer's clock with anti-sniping Highest valid bid at close, then the seller's explicit acceptance (Auctions)
    reverse_l1 GeM reverse auction after technical evaluation Descending bids. Bidders see the current L1 price only, never who bid it. Lowest bid at close
    dutch Perishables, end-of-day clearance Published non-increasing price ticks First conforming acceptance in the auctioneer's chain order
    scheme Bank offers, coupons, QPS "buy 10 get 1", slab and target schemes No bargaining. The buyer asserts machine-readable schemes with eligibility proofs. The seller agrees or disagrees per scheme, and stacking rules apply. Seller Agree per scheme, then a normal accept

    A coupon code on a plain checkout session is not scheme mode: it is a code that the seller applies or rejects (Discounts). Discounts that depend on the payment instrument or on a buyer assertion stay in scheme mode.

    A sealed_first_price auction is negotiated under mode rfq_sealed, and a mandate that allows sealed auctions MUST list rfq_sealed in modes_allowed.

    Capability advertisement¶

    A BPP advertises bacp.negotiation as a provider tag in on_search, mirrored into its profile. The BAP declares its own set in /.well-known/bacp (see Discovery and Profiles). The schema is negotiation-session.json#/$defs/Capability:

    Field Meaning
    negotiable_issues[] JSON Pointers into the term sheet, e.g. /lines/*/unit_price, /payment/credit_days
    modes[] Supported modes
    max_rounds, round_timeout Round limits
    min_offer_validity Shortest validity the advertiser will issue
    sig_algs[], firm_quote_supported Signing algorithms; whether firm quotes are offered
    accepted_handlers[] Payment handlers the seller accepts, which feed the handler intersection
    pricing_ai_vendor Any third-party pricing or negotiation AI the participant uses

    Rules:

    • A session MUST use the intersection of the two adverts.
    • Any issue not in the intersected negotiable_issues is fixed. A counter-offer that changes a fixed issue MUST be refused with NEG_ISSUE_NOT_NEGOTIABLE.
    • max_rounds defaults to 10 for B2C and 30 for B2B.
    • min_offer_validity has a floor of PT5M in every profile. A counterparty's advert MAY lengthen it and MUST NOT shorten it: the effective value is the larger of PT5M and the declared value.
    • An empty mode intersection returns capabilities_incompatible, and the agent falls back to fixed.

    The negotiation mandate¶

    A negotiation is bounded by a negotiation mandate that is separate from the checkout mandate described in Mandates and Consent.

    Why separate. AP2- and Verifiable-Intent-compatible verifiers fail closed on constraint types they do not know. Adding negotiation constraints to the checkout or payment mandate would make those verifiers reject it. BACP therefore carries the bacp.neg.* constraints in their own mandate:

    • vct: in.bacp.mandate.negotiation.1, kind: negotiation, schema mandate-negotiation.json;
    • signed by the principal on the Trusted Surface and countersigned by the Mandate Manager, like every other mandate;
    • for role: buyer, linked to the open checkout mandate by checkout_mandate_ref, which MUST be present. For role: seller (a reverse-auction bidder, a farmer selling a lot), checkout_mandate_ref MUST be absent, because a seller has no checkout mandate.

    Both mandates are evaluated, and the stricter bound wins: the checkout mandate's budgets, allow-lists and step-up rules still apply to a negotiated deal.

    Autonomous acceptance. process.accept_confirmation says what happens when the guard returns acceptable and the agent calls accept.

    • ask is the meaning of an absent key. The gateway raises a confirm step-up with reason accept_confirmation, whose ts_render carries offer_hash, and sends nothing seller-visible before negotiation.step_up.approved.
    • autonomous lets the gateway send the accept without the principal. It takes effect only if the mandate also carries a usable process.notice_route (type mm_event, tel or sms, and ref) that the Mandate Manager writes from a number or reference it has verified as the principal's own (for tel and sms, the number the Trusted Surface holds for the principal, as for voice), and never from a request. A MandateRequest whose proposed constraints carry notice_route is refused with 400 invalid_request at that path (mandate-request.notice-route.invalid.json). mm_event is not usable until BACP defines the event. Otherwise the gateway treats the mandate as ask and records autonomous_unavailable_no_notice_route (info). The only gateway-to-Trusted-Surface channel that BACP defines is the content-free app_push of a step-up (ts_notify_url), which carries a step_up_id and an expiry and never the text of a notice, so it is not a notice_route. BACP defines no gateway-to-Trusted-Surface notice event (Q-61), so a route the gateway itself uses (tel or sms) is the way to meet the condition until one is defined; if one is defined, it becomes an allowed mm_event value and the fallback fires less often.
    • Never preselected. The Mandate Manager MUST write the key on every mandate it issues from the effective date of this version. The notice that the principal approves on the Trusted Surface at set-up MUST present ask and autonomous with equal weight and preselect neither, MUST say that under autonomous the agent may accept a firm offer without the principal when the guard returns acceptable, and MUST give the fixed consequence sentence of each acceptance_revocation value (Render input), so that the principal knows what such an acceptance means before any is sent. In a consumer session this is also the explicit and affirmative action that Rule 4(9) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 asks for ("no such entity shall record such consent automatically"). Whether one Trusted-Surface-signed mandate is consent for each later purchase is open (Q-52).
    • agri seller. In an agri session a seller gateway treats the key as ask whatever the mandate says, so that a farmer always taps their own acceptance. This is a BACP gateway rule, not a legal requirement.
    • Legacy mandates. A mandate with no accept_confirmation is read as ask. A verifier that fails closed on unknown constraint keys refuses a mandate that carries the key until it supports it.
    • No hold. BACP has no accept_hold. The stops are the withdrawal of a purchase offer (Withdrawal) and the principal's decline of an AGREED or BOUND deal (Decline).

    The negotiation mandate is verified only by the principal's own guard and credential provider. It is never disclosed to the counterparty. It travels to the seller only as a within_bounds attestation (see Binding to checkout).

    constraints.negotiation ($defs/NegotiationConstraints) holds every bound. Unknown keys fail closed.

    Reservation price¶

    The reservation price is the principal's private walk-away value.

    • price.reservation is an SD-JWT selectively disclosable claim with sd: true, disclosure: "never" (a constant) and held_by ∈ policy_engine | trusted_surface.
    • It MUST NOT be disclosed to the counterparty, to any seller-side verifier, to a payment processor or to the LLM. The model receives only the guard verdict.
    • Its relation to total_max is:
    Offered all_in_total (buyer) Guard outcome
    At or better than reservation acceptable. The agent may accept without the principal.
    Between reservation and total_max needs_escalation, reason negotiation_between_reservation_and_max. The principal decides on the Trusted Surface.
    Beyond total_max needs_escalation, reason negotiation_out_of_mandate. The agent MUST NOT emit a firm counter-offer or bid at this value. A principal approval here is a fresh closed approval with an RBI-compliant factor.

    For a seller mandate the directions are reversed (total_min is the floor).

    A mandate that sets disclosure to anything other than never, or a buyer mandate without a reservation, is invalid:

    Presence of a negotiation¶

    The gateway derives the presence of a negotiation from the session in which the principal reached the agent, or from an hp_proof (Presence proof); it does not take it from the presence field of NegotiationCreate as the agent states it. A NegotiationCreate that asserts presence: HP without a valid hp_proof is refused with 403 presence_not_proven (requires_buyer_review), and nothing is sent to the seller. HNP needs no proof.

    Term sheet¶

    Every binding offer carries a multi-issue term sheet, schema termsheet.json. Amounts are integer paise in INR.

    Issue envelope. Every negotiable issue is wrapped as {value, negotiable, status, proposed_by}, where status ∈ Assert | Agree | Disagree | Terminate. This generalises ONDC's per-term status handshake for payment terms to every issue. The envelope and the groups are closed (additionalProperties: false), so a reservation value, a utility weight or any undeclared field cannot ride inside a term sheet. Extra issues go only under namespaced extensions, and the guard fails closed on any extension issue that is not in Agree.

    Group Issues
    Price lines[], charges[], all_in_total unit_price; tax_inclusive; gst_rate_pct; read-only mrp; retail_package_mrp_required; price_breaks[]; line_total; every charge with mandatory and first_disclosed_round. all_in_total is required from round 1 and MUST equal Beckn quote.price. pricing_basis ∈ list, volume, negotiated, personalised, auction, scheme, posted_dynamic.
    Quantity lines[] quantity, unit (each, kg, quintal, set, hour, …), moq, tolerance_pct, option_clause_pct (GeM-style ± quantity option), price_breaks[]

    Credit terms and MSMED limits¶

    If the seller is a Udyam micro or small enterprise in manufacturing or services, every credit offset (credit_days and every tranche offset_days) MUST be at most 45 days from acceptance or deemed acceptance. MSMED Act, 2006, s.15 says the period agreed in writing "in no case … shall exceed forty-five days from the day of acceptance or the day of deemed acceptance". The term-sheet schema encodes this rule, and the validator also runs in code.

    • Where no written agreement exists, payment is due within 15 days of acceptance: before the "appointed day" of s.2(b).
    • The day of acceptance is the day of actual delivery, or, if the buyer objects in writing within 15 days of delivery, the day the supplier removes the objection. With no written objection within 15 days, the day of deemed acceptance is the day of actual delivery (s.2(b), Explanation).
    • Medium enterprises are never covered: the Act's "supplier" is a micro or small enterprise (s.2(n)).
    • Traders. The Ministry of MSME Office Memorandum of 02-07-2021 lets retail and wholesale traders register on Udyam but says their benefits "are to be restricted to Priority Sector Lending only". BACP therefore does not apply the cap to a trader seller, and the validator only warns. Whether that memorandum excludes traders from s.15 as a matter of law is open (Q-21).
    • A firm offer that breaches the cap is blocked with NEG_MSMED_CREDIT_CAP. A buyer's own draft only warns.
    • A buyer-signed GRN, or seller-posted delivery evidence that the buyer counter-signed or that carries a third-party reference the buyer can check, dates acceptance for the on_acceptance event and for every MSMED-applicable sale; seller-only evidence dates nothing (MSME late-payment interest). The acceptance date that starts the clock is the delivery date, or the date an objection recorded within 15 days of delivery was removed. How an objection travels on the network is open (Q-21); the rule is pending counsel (Q-83).
    • The credit ledger computes the statutory due date, compound interest at three times the bank rate on overdue amounts (s.16), and the income-tax deduction flag for late payments to micro and small enterprises. See Credit and Split Tender.

    Messages¶

    All negotiation messages are defined by offer.json, a oneOf discriminated by type.

    type Sent by Purpose
    offer Either side A fresh offer (no in_reply_to)
    counter_offer Either side Rejects the live offer and makes a new one. MUST cite the live offer in in_reply_to.
    bid Bidder An auction bid (auction_id required)
    accept Offeree Absolute acceptance of exactly one live offer. Carries no terms.
    reject Offeree Rejects the live offer with a typed reason_code. final: true ends the negotiation.
    withdraw Offeror Revokes the offeror's own offer before acceptance is complete
    terminate Either side or auctioneer Ends the negotiation (max_rounds, deadline, principal_instruction, mandate_revoked, no_agreement, auction_closed_no_award, rfq_not_awarded)
    request_extension Offeree Asks the offeror to extend expires_at. Silence means no extension.
    accept_revoke Acceptor Revokes an acceptance before it reaches the proposer, only where the offer allows it
    bid_commit, bid_reveal, bid_retract Bidder Sealed-bid commitment and reveal; open-outcry retraction (Auctions)

    Common envelope¶

    Every message carries msg_id, negotiation_id, transaction_id (the Beckn context.transaction_id), round, dispatched_at, received_at (filled by the receiver), from{role, np_id, agent_id, offeror_kind}, signatures[] and, once received, counter_signature. Every firm offer, bid, accept, withdraw, accept_revoke, bid_commit and bid_reveal also carries sender_log_seq, sender_log_prev and protocol_terms{version, hash} inside its signed bytes (Signed seller acceptance and clock skew). The gateway fills them; a model never supplies them. An "offer" for these rules includes a bid and an RFQ quotation, on receipt as well as on draft.

    • Signing. A message MUST carry the sending network participant's registry signature (role: bap or bpp): a detached JWS over the JCS of the message without signatures, counter_signature and received_at. The gateway MUST NOT hold or use an agent's key. The agent's own act is evidenced by its RFC 9421 request signature under the tag bacp-negotiate, which the gateway verifies before parsing and records in the transcript (see Identity and Request Signing). A message MAY also carry an agent signature entry. Whether agents SHOULD sign message bodies is open (Q-48).
    • Hashes. msg_hash and offer_hash are sha-256 over JCS of the message minus signatures, counter_signature and received_at, so the hash does not change on receipt. dispatched_at, sender_log_seq, sender_log_prev and protocol_terms are inside the hashed bytes.
    • Receipt. The receiver returns a CounterSignature, a compact JWS by its registry key whose payload is a CounterSignaturePayload (msg_hash, negotiation_id, received_at, log_seq, log_entry_hash, prev_entry_hash, all REQUIRED), in the synchronous response to the exchange. The CounterSignature is a BACP artefact, not a Beckn signed acknowledgement, and BACP does not require Beckn 2.0 signed acknowledgements on the network leg (settled in Q-43). A counterparty that does not return valid CounterSignatures is treated as not supporting firm negotiation: the gateway MUST send it only non-firm offers (firm: false).
    • Agent disclosure. from.offeror_kind ∈ agent | human MUST be present on every message and MUST be shown to the principal.
    • Advisory text. Free text travels only in advisory{binding: false, text_hash, chat_ref}. Counterparty free text reaches the agent only as a messages[] entry with code untrusted_counterparty_text, which the agent framework MUST treat as data. It MUST NOT invoke tools, change mandates or trigger an acceptance.

    Acceptance¶

    An accept carries no terms: the schema forbids term_sheet and in_reply_to. It carries:

    • offer_hash, which MUST equal the hash of the live offer exactly;
    • negotiation_mandate_ref{id, hash}, whose content is never disclosed;
    • legal_effect ∈ acceptance (the accepted offer was firm) | purchase_offer (it was non-firm);
    • bound_attestation, the within_bounds attestation (see Binding to checkout).

    An accept MUST NOT carry a step_up_id or any other field that reveals that the deal needed the principal's approval.

    Notice of an autonomous act. After every accept that the gateway dispatches without a step-up, for buyer and seller roles alike, the gateway emits to the principal the message agreed_by_agent (type: info, resolution: recoverable). For a consumer purchase offer, where the accept is of a non-firm offer, the message is purchase_offered_by_agent. These are info and not requires_buyer_review so that a model does not read them as an instruction to stop and turn autonomous back into ask by its own branching. The notice:

    • is built from gateway templates in the principal's language, never from agent prose, and reaches the principal by the mandate's notice_route, never through the agent's own output;
    • states the seller or buyer name, offeror_kind, the all-in total (in ₹ with Indian grouping, and spoken in words on a voice surface), the quantity and unit, the current-stage cancellation charge, the stop route available in that state, revoke_route, grievance_contact and a content_hash;
    • carries no URI other than those routes and no urgency text (Render input);
    • is recorded in the evidence bundle as notice_renders[], the gateway-signed render with the receipt of its delivery (Evidence).

    The stop button of a purchase_offered_by_agent notice appears only where a carrier exists that can honour it. On a Beckn leg no carrier exists in this version (Beckn / ONDC Binding), so the notice there MUST NOT show a stop control, because a control that cannot work is a misleading interface.

    Withdrawal of a purchase offer (ICA s.5). In a consumer session every autonomous act is a purchase offer, and s.5 lets the buyer revoke a proposal "at any time before the communication of its acceptance is complete as against the proposer". The buyer therefore MAY send a withdraw that carries accept_msg_hash, the msg_hash of its own accept with legal_effect: purchase_offer, until the seller's on_init receipt. The session moves PURCHASE_OFFERED → NEGOTIATING.

    • It is allowed only when the named accept has legal_effect: purchase_offer. A withdraw that names an accept with legal_effect: acceptance is refused with accept_revoke_not_allowed.
    • The agent MAY send it without a presence proof, because it is the buyer-favourable direction. The principal MAY also trigger it by the Trusted Surface event negotiation.purchase_offer.withdrawn.
    • It is free: it is a revocation and not a cancellation, so no stage charge attaches. The seller's application shows "buyer withdrew before you confirmed".
    • A crossing with the seller's on_init is decided by the committed-order rule.
    • On Beckn the carrier (an init withdrawn before on_init) is not yet defined (Q-22). Until it is, the principal's only exit on that leg is the decline below.

    Decline of an AGREED or BOUND deal¶

    No exit from AGREED or BOUND exists on the agent's instruction, and none is added: an agent path would let an agent cancel many formed contracts and leave the charges with the principal. The principal declines on the Trusted Surface with the distinct event negotiation.agreed.declined, signed with its Presentation (Presentation record). The event name differs from negotiation.step_up.declined, because one verb with two consequences cannot be told apart on a voice channel or in the bundle. An agent terminate after AGREED without that event is refused with 403 presence_not_proven.

    • The render MUST show the current-stage charge under the term sheet's cancellation.terms before the factor is taken.
    • The edges are AGREED → CANCELLED and BOUND → CANCELLED, reason principal_declined.
    • It is not a breach when the terms permit cancellation at that stage: it is performance of a term. A breach is flagged, as a claim only and never as a finding, when the terms make that stage non-cancellable or the charge goes unpaid. If the terms are silent on the stage before CONTRACTED, the record carries principal_declined with the flag no_stage_term and no fee is computed.
    • A session already in BREACH_FLAGGED keeps its no-penalty buyer cancel.
    • For a consumer, Rule 4(8) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (parity) bears on any charge; whether it limits a charge before CONTRACTED is open (Q-52). The Rules give no cooling-off right.

    A reject carries a typed reason_code (price, delivery, payment_terms, quality, not_negotiable, mrp_exceeded, msmed_credit_cap, terms_drift, out_of_mandate, incoterm_not_supported, other) and MAY carry an ONDC error code such as 50005. It MUST NOT carry the reservation value.

    Extension requests¶

    Expiry is a signed term, so an extension is a new offer: the offeror re-issues identical terms with a later expires_at and extends: <old hash>, and the old offer becomes SUPERSEDED. If the offeror does not answer, there is no extension.

    request_extension is sent only on the principal's or agent's explicit instruction: a Trusted Surface negotiation.request_extension event, or an explicit agent message. The gateway MUST NOT send it automatically when a negotiation is escalated (see Anti-leak rules). Whether a seller can answer within ONDC's PT30S TTL is open (Q-34).

    The guard¶

    The guard is a deterministic, non-LLM policy engine inside the gateway's trust zone. The model proposes and the guard decides. It runs on every outgoing firm counter-offer, acceptance, bid and award, and on every incoming offer, where it computes the verdict shown to the model. It fails closed, and the first failing step wins. Step-ups are collected so that the principal's diff card shows every out-of-bound issue.

    Step Check On failure
    N-1 Request signature with tag bacp-negotiate, agent credential, both mandates ACTIVE, presence allowed 401 / 403 as for checkout
    N-2 Schema validation of the message and the term sheet. Unknown constraint keys and extension issues not in Agree fail. NEG_SCHEMA_INVALID. Where the failing schema rule encodes a validator (MSMED cap, no-cost EMI), the validator's code is reported instead (NEG_MSMED_CREDIT_CAP, NEG_EMI_NO_COST_FALSE).
    N-3 Capability: mode in the intersection and in modes_allowed; changed issues within negotiable_issues; round ≤ max_rounds; now < deadline capabilities_incompatible, NEG_ISSUE_NOT_NEGOTIABLE, or TERMINATED
    N-4 Offer liveness: in_reply_to or offer_hash is the live hash; the offer has not expired NEG_STALE_OFFER, NEG_OFFER_EXPIRED
    N-5 Validators Per validator: block, step-up or flag
    N-6 Every per-issue bound in constraints.negotiation; counterparty allow-list needs_escalation; mandate_scope_mismatch for a counterparty outside the allow-list
    N-7 Exposure across all of the principal's concurrent negotiations needs_escalation with NEG_EXPOSURE_EXCEEDED
    N-8 Price against the reservation needs_escalation
    N-9 Checkout-mandate rules: category, merchant and PIN code allow-lists, step_up.above_amount, step_up.new_merchant, and the negotiation mandate's new_counterparty_above needs_escalation
    N-10 Payment feasibility: the handler intersection is non-empty for the selected payment option at this presence handlers_incompatible
    N-11 Write a guard entry to the instruction log with constraint IDs only, never values. Request the within_bounds attestation. n/a

    Outcomes¶

    The guard produces one of three verdicts, and the verdict is the only signal about the mandate that reaches the model (live_offer.guard):

    Guard result Verdict shown to the model Meaning
    pass acceptable Within every bound and at or better than the reservation. The agent may act without the principal.
    escalate needs_escalation Outside a bound, between the reservation and the ceiling, over exposure, or a step-up rule fired. Only the principal can approve.
    fail not_acceptable A validator blocked, or a hard rule failed. The agent MUST NOT accept.

    Counter-offers follow two rules:

    • An agent MUST NOT emit a firm counter-offer outside the mandate, because the counterparty could accept it.
    • If the guard returns needs_escalation, the agent MAY still send the counter-offer, but only with subject_to_principal_approval: true, which forces firm: false. If process.firm_counter_allowed is false, every outgoing counter-offer is non-firm.

    Step-up to the Trusted Surface¶

    A negotiation step-up is created only on an outgoing accept or firm-counter attempt. An incoming verdict only sets live_offer.guard; it creates no step-up, so that fifty incoming offers cannot send the principal fifty prompts. On needs_escalation for such an attempt, or on acceptable under accept_confirmation: ask, the session moves to ESCALATED and carries a step_up object with kind: confirm and reason ∈ negotiation_out_of_mandate | negotiation_between_reservation_and_max | accept_confirmation. In the A2A binding this maps to the task state TASK_STATE_AUTH_REQUIRED (A2A 1.0), and in MCP to insufficient_scope (see MCP and A2A).

    • The diff card (issue, offered value, the principal's own bound, all-in total, offer expiry) is rendered only on the Trusted Surface. The agent and the counterparty never receive the bound. In the REST view, step_up.diff[] carries only issue_path, offered and within_bounds.
    • The step-up's ts_render carries the offer_hash of the offer to approve and, for a firm offer, its acceptance_revocation value, which the Trusted Surface shows only as a fixed consequence sentence followed by the cancellation terms (Render input).
    • The Trusted Surface posts the principal's approval as a negotiation.step_up.approved event, never step_up.approved. Its presentation MUST carry offer_hash and MUST NOT carry checkout_hash, because no quote exists yet. The gateway refuses the event with 403 ts_render_mismatch unless presentation.offer_hash equals the ts_render's offer_hash and the offer the step-up names (Presentation record).
    • The principal approves with an RBI-compliant factor such as the UPI PIN or a device biometric. The Mandate Manager then issues a one-off within_bounds attestation bound to that exact offer_hash.
    • The step-up link lives for min(15 minutes, expires_at − now). An offer that expires while the negotiation is ESCALATED cannot be revived by the approval. The approval can then be used only for a fresh offer, and an acceptance attempt returns NEG_OFFER_EXPIRED.
    • Fatigue limits.
      • At most one pending negotiation step-up exists per negotiation_id. It is marked superseded when its offer is superseded, and an event for it gets 409 NEG_STALE_OFFER.
      • At most one step-up is presented at a time per principal. Others queue and expire with their offers.
      • The gateway caps the step-ups it creates per principal. The cap, a count over a window, is declared in /.well-known/bacp (negotiation.step_up_cap); BACP sets no number. Beyond it the gateway answers the agent 429 with Retry-After, and nothing is visible to the seller: a capped offer lapses by silence. The cap also counts the fee-line review prompts of TS-3 and a BACP step-up for the convenience-fee disclosure that NPCI/UPI/CC/01 para 7.5 requires (Mandates and Consent).
      • negotiation.step_up.declined MAY carry end_negotiation: true, which sends terminate with reason principal_instruction, so that declining stays as easy as approving.
      • The Central Consumer Protection Authority's Dark Patterns Guidelines name "nagging" as "repeated and persistent interactions … unless specifically permitted by the user", and from 01-01-2027 Rule 4(15) of the E-Commerce Rules makes compliance and a yearly self-audit a duty of the entity.
    • An offer that lapses while the principal approves produces offer_expired_during_approval in the ts_render language ("the offer ran out while you were approving"), never "you declined".
    • A negotiation approval that names a checkout step-up is refused with 403 ts_render_mismatch, and the session is unchanged.
    • A seller's acceptance of the buyer's non-firm offer is the seller's purchase offer, not a contract. It escalates, and it binds only after the principal approves on the Trusted Surface and the terms are within the mandate's bounds.

    Anti-leak rules¶

    The principal's bounds MUST NOT leak through any channel:

    1. No seller-visible escalation signal. Escalation MUST NOT emit anything the seller can see. In particular the gateway MUST NOT send an automatic request_extension when it escalates, and an accept MUST NOT carry a step_up_id or any approval reference. An extension is requested only on an explicit principal or agent instruction.
    2. Nothing seller-facing carries a bound. No offer, reject reason, error, webhook, Beckn message or evidence bundle shared with the counterparty carries reservation, total_max, total_min, utility weights, or an AP2 amount_range.max. The seller verifies only the within_bounds attestation.
    3. The model does not see the bound. The reservation lives in the policy engine or on the Trusted Surface. The model receives only acceptable, not_acceptable or needs_escalation.
    4. Logs carry IDs, not values. Guard entries carry constraint IDs only. Gateways and registries MUST NOT log term-sheet contents in the clear.
    5. Data-loss prevention. Participants SHOULD scan every buyer-originated outgoing message, response, webhook and log line for the reservation and ceiling values. The scan MUST be provenance-aware: a number that the counterparty sent first and that the buyer side merely echoes (for example, a seller's COD total that happens to equal the reservation) is not a leak. Only buyer-originated content is judged. Conformance runs apply this scan after every case (see Conformance).

    Drift detection¶

    After acceptance, the seller's BPP-signed on_init (and later confirm / on_confirm) MUST carry exactly the accepted terms, apart from payment-rail endpoints. The receiving gateway MUST check this from content, not from claims:

    1. Recompute the term-sheet hash from the term sheet carried in on_init and compare it with the hash of the accepted offer's term sheet. A claimed hash is checked too, but a matching claim is never sufficient.
    2. Compare every line of the bound term sheet with the accepted one on (item_id, unit, quantity, unit_price, currency).
    3. Compare every line item of the BPP-signed quote with the accepted lines on (item, quantity, unit_price, line total).
    4. Compare the quote total with the accepted all_in_total.

    A difference in any one of these is drift, even when the totals still match: a seller that halves the quantity and doubles the unit price, or switches kg for quintal, has drifted.

    Accepted offer On drift
    Firm The accepted firm offer is a contract, so drift is a breach, not a return to negotiation. The session moves to BREACH_FLAGGED with message NEG_TERMS_DRIFT (requires_buyer_review). The gateway raises an IGM 2.0 issue with the agent as an AI_AGENT actor (Proposed, Q-11) and the evidence (accepted hash, claimed and recomputed hashes, the list of differences, both totals). The buyer MAY cancel without penalty (cancelled_no_penalty). A post-acceptance "out of stock" on a firm offer is also a breach. Whether a breach carries a network penalty is open (Q-22).
    Non-firm (purchase offer) The differing on_init is the seller's counter-offer. The accepted offer becomes SUPERSEDED, the session returns to NEGOTIATING, and the buyer gets seller_counter_at_init (requires_buyer_input).

    The gateway records bound_term_sheet_hash as the hash it computed from the bound content, never the hash the seller claimed.

    Signed seller acceptance and clock skew¶

    Transcript positions decide contract formation. Times are recorded, and never decide it.

    A rule that compared timestamps stamped by the offeree itself would leave the offeree a free option inside the 5-second band: it could sign an acceptance, hold it, watch the market, and send it late. BACP therefore decides a crossing only on a signed admission by the party that loses by it, read inside that party's own chain, so no comparison of two parties' clocks decides anything.

    • One chain per participant. Each participant keeps one linearisable hash chain per negotiation_id, like the exposure store. A gateway that runs as several instances MUST share a linearisable chain. Two receipts at one log_seq that name different entries are equivocation: evidence_unverifiable, treated as contested and reported to NEG_COLLUSION_SCREEN.
    • Positions in the signed bytes. A sender logs a firm message and takes its position before it dispatches it. The message carries sender_log_seq (its position in the sender's own chain) and sender_log_prev (the hash of the entry before it) inside its signed bytes. Genesis. At position 1 of either chain, sender_log_prev and the receipt's prev_entry_hash are the genesis hash, sha-256 of the negotiation_id as UTF-8 bytes, written sha-256:<base64url>; this mirrors the InstructionLog genesis. Both chains use the same value, so a verifier recomputes it from the negotiation_id alone.
    • The receipt. Every receiver returns, in the synchronous response to the exchange, a CounterSignature whose payload is a CounterSignaturePayload: {msg_hash, negotiation_id, received_at, log_seq, log_entry_hash, prev_entry_hash}, every field REQUIRED. prev_entry_hash lets a fork be proved from two receipts alone. log_entry_hash changes from MAY to REQUIRED.

    Contested crossings¶

    Write W for the offeror's withdraw and A for the offeree's accept. Each comparison sits inside one party's own chain.

    Outcome Condition Code Who proved it
    Withdraw wins The offeree's CounterSignature for W has log_seq below A's sender_log_seq (the offeree logged W before it dispatched A) accept_void_withdrawn (seller acceptor: seller_accept_void) The offeree, the loser
    Accept wins The offeror's CounterSignature for A has log_seq below W's sender_log_seq (the offeror received A before it sent W) withdraw_lost_crossing The offeror, the loser
    Contested Neither holds (each sent before it received), or a needed receipt is missing, late or unverifiable crossing_contested Nobody; humans decide
    Impossible Both hold evidence_unverifiable, treated as contested and reported to NEG_COLLUSION_SCREEN Equivocation

    Why contested is the only rule that does not reward holding. A genuine crossing (A sent at position 5, W sent at position 10 a moment before A arrives) produces the same four integers as an acceptance that the offeree signed and logged, did not send, and sent later after seeing W: no signed artefact tells them apart. Any rule that decides the genuine case for one side therefore also rewards the hold. A contested outcome gives the holder a review and never a contract.

    Residual risk, disclosed. An offeror that holds the CounterSignature for A, sends W, and only then logs A turns a contract it owed into a contested review. The payoff is "no deal, no breach", the window is the 5-second receipt bound, and it cannot be prevented without a third-party sequencer. Every contested record carries late_receipt_party, and gateways report per-participant contested counts to the collusion screen. BACP sets no threshold.

    What a contested crossing does. BACP evidence does not settle formation; neither gateway proceeds; the protocol records no breach event. BACP does not say "no contract", because a protocol term under the Information Technology Act, 2000, s.13 can fix when a record is despatched and received but cannot declare that no contract exists; whether one formed is a question of fact under ICA ss.4-5.

    • Each side's session goes to ESCALATED with reason crossing_contested, and both principals are asked on their own surfaces. The seller gateway MUST raise the case to its principal and MUST NOT auto-confirm (the fail-safe of Indian Contract Act timing is two-sided).
    • Both confirm → AGREED, and the deal proceeds.
    • Neither confirms (declined or silent) by the earlier of the offer's expires_at and the end of the step-up link → CANCELLED, reason crossing_unconfirmed. No breach event, no protocol-computed charge, and the exposure reservation is released once (Principle 6; TS-2, "expiry means decline").
    • One confirms, the other declines or is silent → CANCELLED, reason formation_disputed, carrying the grievance_contact of both sides. No breach event and no protocol-computed charge. Whether a contract formed is for ODR or the courts. Silence is a decline, so a deal never proceeds on one confirmation.
    • The message is crossing_contested with resolution requires_buyer_review on a buyer-side gateway and requires_seller_review on a seller-side gateway (Errors). The agent MUST stop and MUST NOT resend.
    • On Beckn a message is ordered only when its Ack carries a verifying receipt, so every crossing that depends on an unreceipted Beckn message is contested. Non-crossing deals are unaffected.

    Expiry and the acceptor's margin¶

    expires_at is a receipt deadline at the offeror, stated in the protocol terms and in the offer semantics. An accept received after expires_at returns NEG_OFFER_EXPIRED. Two guards stop an offeror from escaping its own offer at the expiry edge:

    • Acceptor's margin. The acceptor's gateway MUST NOT dispatch an accept when expires_at − now is under 5 seconds on its own clock. It refuses locally with NEG_OFFER_EXPIRED and sends nothing, and the principal sees offer_expired_during_approval. An honest acceptor loses the last 5 seconds of each offer's validity. This margin is the acceptor's own, not an extra check at the offeror.
    • Dispatch before expiry. An accept that is refused as expired, whose dispatched_at is not after expires_at, and which passed freshness, is crossing_contested, not expired. A self-asserted time can move this case only to contested, never to bound. An accept received with dispatched_at after expires_at is NEG_OFFER_EXPIRED.

    Whether a court reads an acceptance dispatched before expires_at but received after it as in time (ICA s.4, s.6(2)) is open (Q-52).

    Per-principal exposure¶

    An agent that sends firm counter-offers to several sellers at once could commit the principal several times over. The guard therefore counts exposure (step N-7):

    • Exposure is the sum of all_in_total over the principal's outstanding firm counter-offers, ACCEPT_PENDING acceptances, AGREED-but-unpaid deals and lodged standing accepts (each at max_price × qty), across all of that principal's negotiation mandates and concurrent negotiations, including every session under the same rfq_parent_id, plus the message being guarded.
    • Exposure MUST be at most total_max and at most every remaining checkout-mandate budget period, whatever the payment handler. A credit-ledger deal counts against the budgets and against credit_ledger.max_outstanding only when a gateway complete books it (Q-46, settled).
    • Over the limit, the verdict is needs_escalation with NEG_EXPOSURE_EXCEEDED.
    • Atomicity. Computing exposure and emitting the firm message it guards MUST run as one atomic, linearisable operation keyed on the principal. Each outstanding commitment holds an exposure reservation. A standing accept's reservation is keyed on its standing_accept_id (it has no offer_hash) and is released when its auction closes or the standing accept is used or withdrawn. Any other reservation is released when its offer reaches REJECTED, EXPIRED, WITHDRAWN, SUPERSEDED, TERMINATED or LAPSED_CONDITION, or ACCEPT_REVOKED on the proposer's countersigned accept_revoke_honoured, and converted when the deal is paid. Release is idempotent: it is keyed on the offer's offer_hash (a standing accept's, on its standing_accept_id) and happens at most once per reservation, however often the event that releases it is delivered or replayed. While the outcome of an accept_revoke is contested (the proposer has not countersigned an outcome), the reservation is held. If the outcome is still contested when the offer's expires_at passes, the gateway surfaces it to the principal (requires_buyer_review) and the reservation stays counted. A gateway that runs as several instances MUST share a linearisable exposure store. Exposure across two different gateways serving one principal is not counted: each open mandate belongs to one gateway (agent.bap_id), and a move to another gateway is a successor root (Q-36, settled).
    • The session view reports only amounts (exposure.principal_outstanding_firm, counted_against[]), never the bound.

    Binding to checkout¶

    Negotiation hands over to checkout through a chain of hashes. BACP does not treat the accepted offer as the checkout:

    1. The buyer accepts offer_hash. The accept carries the within_bounds attestation.
    2. The seller's BPP-signed on_init echoes the identical term sheet, and the gateway's recomputed hash becomes bound_term_sheet_hash. The session is BOUND.
    3. The checkout session opens in ready_for_complete with the BPP-signed quote. Its checkout_hash is the hash of that signed quote.
    4. The closed checkout and payment mandates bind to checkout_hash, as in Sessions and Checkout.

    checkout_hash MUST NOT be asserted to equal offer_hash or bound_term_sheet_hash: they are hashes of different objects (the quote, the offer and the term sheet).

    The within_bounds attestation (offer.json#/$defs/BoundAttestation) is {type: bacp.neg.bound_attestation, mandate_ref, offer_hash, result: true, iss, iat, signature}, issued by the buyer's credential provider (the Mandate Manager). Before issuing it, the credential provider MUST re-verify the deal against the open checkout mandate and the negotiation mandate. The seller BPP MUST verify only this attestation against the issuer's registry key. If the credential provider refuses, the acceptance is not sent and the agent gets NEG_ATTESTATION_REFUSED. Whether a zero-knowledge range proof should replace the attestation is open (Q-24).

    On Beckn, the acceptance is an init carrying bacp.neg.accept, BOUND is the on_init echo, the PO is confirm referencing offer_hash, and PO acceptance is on_confirm (CONTRACTED). The PO MUST NOT alter accepted terms. See Beckn / ONDC Binding.

    Indian Contract Act timing¶

    Legal basis pending review

    The statutory rules cited below are taken from the text of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, ss.2 and 4–7. The timing rules in this section are normative as protocol terms that participants agree to, in the sense of the Information Technology Act, 2000, s.13, and every firm message names them through protocol_terms. Whether courts would treat them, and the crossing rule, which is a BACP design choice, as the time of dispatch and receipt is open (Q-52). Fail-safe default until Q-52 is settled: when a deal's formation is decided by the crossing rule, neither gateway proceeds to init, complete or confirm on it automatically. The buyer gateway escalates to the buyer principal on the Trusted Surface (requires_buyer_review), the seller gateway escalates to the seller principal (requires_seller_review) and MUST NOT auto-confirm, and each proceeds only on its principal's confirmation (Contested crossings). The crossing outcome is still recorded in the transcript.

    Rule Statute BACP behaviour
    Proposal (s.2(a), (b)) A person who signifies willingness to do or abstain from something, "with a view to obtaining the assent of that other", makes a proposal. A proposal, when accepted, becomes a promise. firm: true is a proposal. firm: false is an invitation to treat. That term is not in the Act; it comes from case law. Accepting a non-firm offer has legal_effect: purchase_offer: it is the buyer's proposal, and the seller's echoing on_init or on_confirm is the acceptance.
    Communication (s.4) A proposal is communicated "when it comes to the knowledge of the person to whom it is made". An acceptance is complete against the proposer "when it is put in a course of transmission to him, so as to be out of the power of the acceptor", and against the acceptor "when it comes to the knowledge of the proposer". A proposal is communicated when the offeree's CounterSignature exists. Acceptance is complete against the proposer on dispatch (dispatched_at), and against the acceptor on receipt (received_at).
    Communication of a revocation (s.4) The communication of a revocation is complete "as against the person who makes it, when it is put into a course of transmission to the person to whom it is made, so as to be out of the power of the person who makes it", and "as against the person to whom it is made, when it comes to his knowledge". A withdraw or an accept_revoke is dispatched at its dispatched_at and received at the receiver's received_at.
    Revocation of a proposal (ss.5, 6(1)) A proposal "may be revoked at any time before the communication of its acceptance is complete as against the proposer, but not afterwards". A proposal is revoked "by the communication of notice of revocation by the proposer to the other party". The offeror may withdraw only until the offeree dispatches an acceptance. A withdraw takes effect on receipt.
    Revocation of an acceptance (s.5) "An acceptance may be revoked at any time before the communication of the acceptance is complete as against the acceptor, but not afterwards." Governed by the offer's acceptance_revocation term. See Acceptance revocation.
    Proposal kept open (s.5, Explanation, Uttar Pradesh) Inserted by the Uttar Pradesh Act 57 of 1976: "Where an invitation to a proposal contains a condition that any proposal made in response to such invitation shall be kept open for a specified time and a proposal is thereupon made accepting such condition, such proposal may not be revoked within such time." BACP has no rule for it and does not refuse a withdraw on that ground. Whether it binds a quotation made in response to an RFQ is open (Q-19).
    Crossing (BACP rule) The Act gives no tie-break. When a withdraw and an accept cross, the outcome comes from the four cases of Contested crossings: the withdrawal wins on the offeree's own receipt of it, the acceptance wins on the offeror's own receipt of it, and otherwise the crossing is contested. No timestamp decides it, and there is no tie rule. A losing withdrawal is a breach event and is reported (withdraw_lost_crossing). A losing acceptance is void (accept_void_withdrawn, seller_accept_void). A contested crossing records no breach event.
    Lapse (s.6(2), (3), (4)) A proposal is revoked "by the lapse of the time prescribed in such proposal for its acceptance", "by the failure of the acceptor to fulfil a condition precedent to acceptance", and "by the death or insanity of the proposer, if the fact of his death or insanity comes to the knowledge of the acceptor before acceptance". An offer lapses at expires_at, which is a receipt deadline at the offeror (Expiry), and silence leads to EXPIRED. A counter-offer that cites an expired offer is refused with NEG_OFFER_EXPIRED. Either party MAY send a fresh offer while rounds and the deadline remain. If a conditions_precedent[] entry fails (e.g. lc_opened), the offer becomes LAPSED_CONDITION. Treating every condition precedent as one of the kind s.6(3) describes is a BACP choice. BACP has no message for the case of s.6(4).
    Absolute acceptance (s.7(1)) The acceptance must "be absolute and unqualified". An acceptance mirrors offer_hash and carries no terms. A changed term is a counter-offer. That a counter-offer rejects the prior offer comes from case law, not from the text of s.7.
    Manner of acceptance (s.7(2)) If an acceptance is not made in the prescribed manner, the proposer "may, within a reasonable time after the acceptance is communicated to him, insist" on that manner, "but if he fails to do so, he accepts the acceptance". Every offer prescribes acceptance_manner: bacp.neg.accept. Any other form is non-conforming and is refused promptly with NEG_ACCEPT_NONCONFORMING, so that the proposer insists on the prescribed manner within a reasonable time.

    The Information Technology Act, 2000, s.13 fixes when an electronic record is despatched and received "save as otherwise agreed" between originator and addressee. The protocol terms that every firm message names (protocol_terms{version, hash}) are such an agreed rule. They state the committed-order rule, the receipt window, the contested outcome, that expires_at is a receipt deadline, and the stipulation of s.12(2) that unless an acknowledgement has been received the electronic record is deemed never to have been sent. An individual's agreement is shown on what the person sees: the mandate and the ConsentReceipt cite the terms. Whether a hash an agent sends is the principal's agreement for s.13 (s.11 attribution), and whether a consumer's approval citing the terms is "otherwise agreed", are open (Q-19).

    The protocol-terms text and its hash. The protocol-terms text of a version is the file protocol-terms/<version>.txt published with that version (protocol-terms/2026-10-18.txt for this one), UTF-8 with LF line ends and no byte order mark. hash is sha-256: followed by the unpadded base64url SHA-256 of the exact bytes of that file, with no canonicalisation. A gateway computes it from the published file and never from its own copy of the wording, and a receiver refuses a firm message whose hash differs with capabilities_incompatible. scripts/validate.py checks that every example carries the digest of the file of its version.

    Acceptance revocation¶

    Legal basis pending review

    Whether an offer, an earlier agreement between the parties or a protocol term can validly exclude the acceptor's right under s.5 is open (Q-19). BACP permits waived only under the conditions below. Showing the term to a principal does not make the exclusion valid.

    The Indian Contract Act, 1872, s.5 says: "An acceptance may be revoked at any time before the communication of the acceptance is complete as against the acceptor, but not afterwards." Under s.4 that communication is complete as against the acceptor "when it comes to the knowledge of the proposer". Neither section says "unless otherwise agreed". The Information Technology Act, 2000, s.10A covers "the revocation of proposals and acceptances" expressed in electronic form. Section 13 fixes only when an electronic record is despatched and received, "save as otherwise agreed"; it does not mention knowledge or revocation. BACP takes the proposer's receipt time (received_at) as the time of that knowledge. Whether s.13 receipt is s.4 knowledge is open (Q-19, Q-52).

    Here "acceptance" means acceptance of a proposal (ICA ss.4–5), not acceptance of goods under MSMED s.15.

    The term. Every firm offer MUST carry acceptance_revocation. There is no default, and a gateway MUST NOT insert or normalise the value before hashing, so the term is always inside the signed bytes and offer_hash. A firm offer without it is refused with 400 NEG_SCHEMA_INVALID and never becomes LIVE.

    • allowed_until_receipt. The acceptor may revoke an acceptance until the proposer receives it. In this version no receiver honours this value: every receiver refuses it, on every binding and in every trade profile (table below). The value stays in the schemas so that a later version can lift the refusal without a schema change; the rules that would then apply are the sunset text.
    • waived. The offer excludes that right. It MUST carry waiver_basis: the signed contract between the parties (doc_type: contract) or the buyer's own RFQ (doc_type: rfq, see Award), with its hash. Inside offer_hash this is a commitment to the document, not proof of it. The acceptor's gateway MUST NOT accept a waived offer without the principal unless it holds the document that hashes to waiver_basis.hash; otherwise it escalates to the principal (requires_buyer_review). "Holds" means all three of: the gateway obtained the document from its own principal before the offer arrived; the document names the offer's counterparty, by its np_id or its GSTIN; and the principal marked the document as a waiver basis. If any of the three is missing, the gateway escalates to the principal (requires_buyer_review). A document fetched from waiver_basis.uri, taken from the offer, or supplied by the counterparty in any other way does not count.

    Consumer sessions. The value rules depend on the session's trade profile (context.bacp.profile, see Trade Profiles). A consumer profile is one that the profile table marks "waiver permitted: no". The profile is fixed when the session opens; a later message of the session that declares another profile is refused with capabilities_incompatible and changes nothing. A session is a consumer session, whatever profile it declares, if it declares no profile, if the buyer side's context.bacp.acting_for.kind is individual, or if the buyer side sends no acting_for. The buyer side declares the profile and acting_for at session open in a buyer-signed message, and the buyer gateway fills them from the mandate; a seller refuses a profile that misdescribes the trade as set out in Declaring a profile.

    Sunset text for allowed_until_receipt¶

    Proposed (sunset)

    The rules in this subsection are not in force in this version. They state when a later version may lift the refusal of allowed_until_receipt and how the value is then honoured. The conformance cases that need a live offer with allowed_until_receipt are published as data in the Proposed (sunset) block.

    Conditions for lifting the refusal. A later version lifts the refusal on a binding only when all of these hold:

    • (a) The revoke names its acceptance and offer. accept_revoke carries accept_msg_hash and offer_hash, and a revoke whose dispatched_at is earlier than the dispatched_at of the acceptance it names is void. (Specified in this version.)
    • (b) The receiver commits to receipt order before it sees the next message. In force in this version for every firm negotiation message (Signed seller acceptance and clock skew). The receiver returns its CounterSignature, carrying log_entry_hash and log_seq, in the synchronous response to each negotiation message, before it appends any later message of that negotiation to its transcript. The outcome of an accept_revoke is a separate message. A CounterSignature that reaches the sender more than 5 seconds after the sender's dispatch, on the sender's own monotonic clock, is recorded but does not count as an order proof. A verifier that holds a CounterSignature naming transcript position n for one message and a transcript with a different message at n reports evidence_unverifiable.
    • (c) The matched-pair rule below is implemented, with the hold kept in the transcript.
    • (d) Exposure release on a countersigned accept_revoke_honoured is idempotent (Per-principal exposure). (Specified in this version.)
    • (e) Beckn. A Beckn carrier for accept_revoke exists, or Beckn legs keep refusing the value (Q-22).
    • (f) Tested. The 16 sunset-block cases (T3-08 to T3-37) pass in a harness that loads the conformance YAML. Not met as of 18-10-2026: no implementation passes them yet. It can be met only after an implementation provides the committed-order rule, synchronous receipts, accept_revoke and the matched pair. Even then (e) stays open until a Beckn carrier exists.

    Revocation under allowed_until_receipt (sunset). Call the acceptance A and the revocation R.

    1. R carries accept_msg_hash (the msg_hash of A) and offer_hash (the offer A names). R's dispatched_at MUST NOT be earlier than A's dispatched_at; otherwise R is void.
    2. The proposer orders the two by its transcript (receipt order is chain order), and commits to that order under condition (b): its CounterSignature on each message, with log_entry_hash, is in the synchronous response to that message.
    3. If R is received first, the proposer appends R to the negotiation's transcript on receipt and holds it, keyed on accept_msg_hash, until the offer's expires_at, ordered by chain. Every instance of the proposer that handles A reads the transcript for a held R. If A (same msg_hash and offer_hash) arrives within the hold, the pair matches: the offer moves LIVE → ACCEPT_REVOKED, the negotiation stays in or returns to NEGOTIATING, and the outcome is accept_revoke_honoured. If A does not arrive, R is discarded at the end of the hold and the discard is logged. Discarding an earlier revocation sooner would be narrower than ICA s.5 and its Illustration, so the hold runs to expires_at, ordered by chain, and is not a separate revocation window.
    4. If A is received first, the acceptance stands (AGREED), and the outcome is accept_revoke_after_receipt.
    5. The proposer countersigns and logs every R, whatever the outcome, and sends the outcome code as a separate message. A receipt of A or R without log_entry_hash is not recorded (evidence_unverifiable), and the negotiation does not move to AGREED on it.
    6. The acceptor's offer moves ACCEPTED → ACCEPT_REVOKED, and its negotiation from ACCEPT_PENDING to NEGOTIATING, only on the proposer's countersigned accept_revoke_honoured. Until then the outcome is contested, and the exposure reservation is held.

    Mandate revocation¶

    When the negotiation mandate is revoked, the gateway MUST send withdraw (reason mandate_revoked) for every live firm offer of that principal, within the 60-second revocation target set in Mandates and Consent, and end open sessions TERMINATED. An acceptance that crosses one of these withdrawals follows the crossing rule. If the counterparty accepted earlier, the deal stands, and the principal is told with agreed_by_counterparty (requires_buyer_review).

No operation in this version lets an agent obtain a step-up that names an accept_revoke, so such a revoke is refused.

    MCP results return the NegotiationSession in structuredContent. A2A 1.0 task states map as: TASK_STATE_WORKING (waiting on the counterparty), TASK_STATE_INPUT_REQUIRED (a counter-offer to answer), TASK_STATE_AUTH_REQUIRED (ESCALATED, including the ask confirmation and a contested crossing), TASK_STATE_COMPLETED (CONTRACTED), TASK_STATE_CANCELED or TASK_STATE_FAILED (REJECTED, TERMINATED, CANCELLED). Versions of A2A before 1.0 use the lowercase names working, input-required, auth-required, completed, canceled and failed. These are A2A's own values and are used verbatim; BACP's own spelling is "cancelled", so A2A TASK_STATE_CANCELED (or canceled) maps to the BACP negotiation state CANCELLED and to the checkout session status cancelled. Agent-to-agent negotiation over A2A without a network participant's signature can produce only firm: false offers.
