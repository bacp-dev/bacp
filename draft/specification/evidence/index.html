    Evidence and Instruction Log¶

    Version 2026-10-18

    This page is part of BACP version 2026-10-18. Rules marked Proposed depend on an open question and may change in a later dated release.

    When an agent buys something on a principal's behalf, every party may later need to show what was authorised, what was instructed, what was offered and what was paid. BACP produces that evidence as a by-product of every transaction, using three artefacts:

    Artefact Schema Produced by Proves
    InstructionLog entries instruction-log-entry.json The agent operator's gateway The ordered chain prompt → intent → cart → mandate → payment → order, tamper-evident
    Receipt receipt.json The verifier that acted: PSP, BPP or Mandate Manager That a payment, refund, checkout or mandate step happened, anchored to the rail
    Evidence bundle EvidenceBundle in the REST binding The gateway, on request Everything above for one transaction, verified and signed, for a dispute

    InstructionLog¶

    The InstructionLog is a signed, append-only, hash-chained record kept per checkout session. It lets a reviewer reconstruct what the principal authorised and what they instructed, without the operator being able to rewrite history afterwards.

    Entry contents¶

    Field Meaning
    seq 0 for the first entry of the session, then +1 per entry, with no gaps.
    session_id, transaction_id The session and the Beckn transaction, end to end.
    stage What kind of step this is (see Stages).
    actor Who performed the step: user, agent, gateway, trusted_surface, bpp, psp, or a session participant: assisted_operator, seller_operator, sub_agent (Human participants).
    actor_ref The participant's participant_id (REQUIRED for assisted_operator, seller_operator and sub_agent), or on a handoff entry the key id of the support operator who accepted or released it.
    at When it happened.
    channel, language For example voice and hi-IN.
    content_hash sha-256 of the raw content (a transcript, audio, a structured intent, a quote, a receipt).
    content_ref Where the operator keeps the encrypted raw content.
    summary A short multilingual summary.
    model The model id, version and, for voice or translated turns, translation_model.
    refs Cross-references: mandate_ref, checkout_hash, message_id, order_id, rrn, receipt_id, issue_id; at stage discover, also idempotency_key, source_digests[], source_checks[] and registry_lookup; at stage cart, discount_codes[] ({code_mac, source}).
    prev_hash, entry_hash The chain links (Hash chain).
    signature The operator's signature.

    Only hashes and references leave the operator. Raw content stays encrypted at the agent operator, and is released only inside an evidence bundle to a party entitled to it (Access).

    Stages¶

    stage Recorded when Typical actor
    ai_disclosure The agent presents the AI disclosure and the routes to a human. It is the first entry whose actor is agent, before intent (Human participants) agent
    prompt The principal instructs the agent (text or voice; voice records channel, language and translation model) user
    intent The agent turns the instruction into a structured intent or mandate request agent
    discover A search is sent and results arrive. The agent records the Idempotency-Key of the search, the source_digest of every discovery source it checked, the outcome of each (verified, mismatch or unverified, with the reason discovery_source_mismatch or discovery_source_unverified) and the host of the registry lookup it used (Verifying discovery results). content_hash covers the discover response as returned agent, gateway
    cart Items are selected; the handler comparison is logged here agent, gateway
    disclosure The BPP's signed quote and disclosures arrive; records checkout_hash and the disclosures hash bpp
    mandate A mandate is activated, used or changes status, including each gateway pause reason (binding_ineligible, reissue_required) added or removed, with the registry_version (Handler binding); records mandate hashes gateway, trusted_surface
    step_up A step-up is requested, approved, declined or expires gateway, trusted_surface
    payment A debit is initiated (basket-blind instruction), a receipt arrives, an in-doubt debit is reconciled, a refund is issued gateway, psp
    order on_confirm creates the order bpp
    post_order Cancels, returns, status changes and post-order refunds agent, assisted_operator, bpp, psp
    grievance An IGM issue is raised and every action on it agent, bpp
    negotiation Every negotiation message and counter-signature agent, bpp
    guard Every negotiation guard verdict (constraint ids only) gateway
    ledger Credit-ledger postings and acknowledgements gateway, seller_operator
    handoff A handoff to a human is requested, accepted by a registered support operator, or released (Human handoff) agent, gateway

    The stage disclosure is the seller's quote; the AI disclosure is always ai_disclosure. The negotiation, guard and ledger stages are described in Negotiation and Credit and Split Tender.

    Signer roles¶

    • Every entry is signed by the agent operator (the gateway's BAP registry key) with signature.role: operator, whatever its actor. The actor field records who acted; the signature records who attests to the record.
    • The signature is asymmetric (ES256 or EdDSA), as a detached JWS over the JCS form of the entry minus signature. A shared-secret MAC MUST NOT be used, because it cannot give non-repudiation.
    • The acts of other parties are proved by their own signatures on the artefacts the entry hashes and references: the BPP's signature on the quote, the PSP's on the Receipt, the principal's and MM's on the mandate, the Trusted Surface's on a step-up event. A human act is never proved by the operator's signature alone: the bundle carries the signed Trusted Surface events themselves (Bundle contents).

    Hash chain¶

    entry_hash = sha-256( JCS( entry minus {entry_hash, signature} ) )
prev_hash  = entry_hash of entry seq - 1
prev_hash of seq 0 (genesis) = sha-256( session_id as UTF-8 bytes )

    Hashes are written sha-256:<base64url>. The log head is the entry_hash of the last entry, or the genesis hash when the log is empty.

    Anchoring. The gateway MUST put the current log_head in context.bacp.log_head on every committing Beckn message it sends (init, confirm, update, cancel, issue). Those messages are signed on the network, so the BPP and the network hold independent copies of the head at each commitment. An operator that deletes or rewrites an entry produces a chain that no longer matches the heads recorded in the network trace.

    Chain verification¶

    A verifier MUST check a log as follows, and MUST reject it at the first failure:

    1. The log is not empty.
    2. Every entry validates against instruction-log-entry.json.
    3. For entry i (counting from 0): seq = i, and session_id equals the first entry's.
    4. prev_hash equals the genesis hash for i = 0, and the previous entry's entry_hash otherwise.
    5. Recomputing entry_hash gives the stored value.
    6. signature verifies against the registry key named by its kid.
    7. If an expected head is known (for example from context.bacp.log_head in a network message, or from the gateway's current state), the last entry_hash equals it.

    The gateway MUST run this check on the session's log before complete. A broken chain returns 403 log_chain_invalid (unrecoverable) and no money moves (Deterministic evaluation).

    Receipts¶

    A Receipt is the signed statement of the party that acted: the payer PSP for a payment or refund, the BPP for a checkout, the Mandate Manager for a mandate. It follows AP2's mandate, checkout and payment receipts and adds the Indian rail references.

    Field Rule
    receipt_type mandate, checkout, payment or refund.
    iss The verifier that acted. A payment or refund receipt MUST be signed with a registry key of role PSP; a checkout receipt with a key of role BPP.
    result success, failure or in_doubt.
    reference The hash of the closed mandate that was presented: sha-256 over the JCS form of the mandate minus status, usage and signatures.
    mandate_ref, open_mandate_ref The closed mandate id and its open parent.
    transaction_id, order_id The Beckn transaction, end to end, and the order.
    checkout_hash, amount, payee Required on a successful payment or refund receipt. amount MUST be greater than zero.
    rail The rail evidence (below).
    refund_state Refund receipts only (below).
    fx Optional foreign-currency view; settlement stays in INR.
    signature The issuer's signature.

    Rail evidence¶

    • rail.type is a handler reference; producers SHOULD emit the canonical id. rail.type, rail.rail_ref and rail.status are always present.
    • UPI family (upi.*, in.upi.*, in.cod.upi, in.pos.tap, in.pos.dynamic_qr): a successful payment or refund receipt MUST carry the 12-digit rrn, and MUST carry utr whenever the handler descriptor's settlement.evidence_refs lists it.
    • Other handlers: the receipt MUST carry at least one of rrn, utr or rail.evidence. rail.evidence is a string map whose keys come from the descriptor's settlement.evidence_refs (for example arn and auth_code for cards, umrn for NACH, voucher_id for e-RUPI, ledger_entry_id for store credit and bacp.credit_ledger, awb for a COD record).
    • rail.evidence MUST NOT contain anything the descriptor lists under data_minimisation.never_collect (PIN, OTP, card number, CVV, expiry, Aadhaar number).
    • Cash on delivery moves no money through a rail and has no payment Receipt; the order records the collection against the shipment awb.

    Rail references let receipts be joined with the rail's own dispute channel (UDIR for UPI). See Handler Registry.

    Refund receipts¶

    A refund receipt MUST be signed by a registered PSP, and the gateway applies it only to a payment of the same checkout_hash and payee. A payment success or failure receipt is applied only when it is signed by the PSP of the closed Payment Mandate's auth_binding.psp_id (HNP: the open Payment Mandate's rail_binding.psp_id), and each receipt_id and rail reference is applied once; otherwise the gateway answers 400 receipt_invalid and the reservation stays (Usage ledger). A refund receipt MUST identify the original payment (original_rrn, original_utr, or an original_* key in rail.evidence), proving the refund went back to the original method, and MUST carry refund_state: refunded_to_source (the default for a completed refund to source), released_block, pending_cardholder_consent, reversed_to_bank_account, credit_note_issued or adjusted_against_dues. A card refund before the bill is paid MUST be adjusted_against_dues and MUST NOT pass through pending_cardholder_consent.

    budget_after is never signed¶

    The PSP holds no mandate budgets. A Receipt MUST NOT contain budget_after, and the schema rejects one that does. The gateway computes the remaining budget from its own ledger after applying the receipt and returns it unsigned in session.payment.budget_after (Mandates). A verifier MUST ignore any budget figure that claims to come from a receipt.

    How receipts are used¶

    • The gateway applies a payment receipt to the mandate ledger only after verifying its signature against a registry key of role PSP and checking that it belongs to the mandate. A forged or mismatched receipt is refused with 400 receipt_invalid.
    • A webhook MUST NOT embed a signed Receipt. It carries a ReceiptRef: receipt_id, receipt_hash (sha-256 over the JCS form of the full signed receipt), receipt_type, result, and where present amount, refund_state and rail {type, rrn, status, original_rrn}.
    • The full signed receipt is available to the party of record by authenticated GET on its owning resource (/sessions/{id}, /orders/{id}, /orders/{id}/returns/{rid}). An agent that relies on it MUST check that its hash equals the receipt_hash it was sent.

    Evidence bundle¶

    GET /evidence/{transaction_id} returns the evidence bundle for a transaction: everything a grievance officer, an ODR body, a regulator or the principal needs to verify the purchase, without trusting the agent or its operator.

    Contents¶

    Field Contents
    transaction_id The Beckn transaction.
    agent_credential_hash Hash of the AgentCredential the agent used.
    mandates[] The open Checkout and Payment Mandates, each wrapped in a JWS by the operator (the payload keeps the principal and MM signatures), and the closed mandates exactly as presented (agent-signed or principal-signed attached JWS).
    checkout The BPP-signed quote (bpp_signed_quote), checkout_hash, disclosures_hash and the disclosures they hash (including catalogue[]), and bpp_signed_payment_terms: the seller's payment terms from on_init (Beckn payments[], with collected_by and the payee VPA in params.virtual_payment_address) as the gateway received them, kept as {body, authorization}: the raw on_init body and its Beckn Authorization header, which verifies against the BPP's registry key (Beckn signing). REQUIRED for every Reserve Pay leg (Sibling Payment Mandates).
    receipts[] The full signed receipts: the BPP's checkout receipt, the PSP's payment receipt, and any refund receipts.
    consent_receipts[] The ConsentReceipts under which data was shared.
    instruction_log[] The session's full InstructionLog.
    discovery_response The signed POST /discover response as {status, body, content_digest, signature_input, signature}, where body is the response content exactly as sent, with no content coding, and its sha-256 is the content_hash of the session's agent-written discover InstructionLog entry. REQUIRED when Disclosures.catalogue[] is present and that entry exists. The verifier rebuilds the signature base from status (@status) and the stored content_digest.
    discovery_sources[] For a session whose items came from POST /discover: the BPP-signed on_search callback behind every source_digest in checkout.disclosures.catalogue[], each as {body, authorization} exactly as the gateway received it (Retention of the signed callbacks). A verifier checks that the sha-256 of each body equals the source_digest and that the authorization verifies against the BPP's registry key, and compares the item values in the body with checkout.disclosures.catalogue[].
    trusted_surface_events[] Every signed Trusted Surface event of the transaction, verbatim as received at /trusted-surface/events (step-up results, mandate activation, credit acceptance, AFA completion), each with its Presentation.
    ts_renders[] The gateway-signed ts_render (compact JWS) of every step-up that a step_up.approved, step_up.declined, negotiation.step_up.approved or negotiation.step_up.declined event in trusted_surface_events[] names, byte-identical to what the Trusted Surface fetched. The content_ref of such an event's presentation is the fragment #/ts_renders/<index>, an RFC 6901 JSON Pointer to the render's position in this array; an index outside the array, or a content_hash that differs from the sha-256 of the render found there, is evidence_unverifiable. In a bundle released to a seller or a payer PSP, a summary step-up's render is left out and ts_render_digests[] carries it instead.
    notice_renders[] For every autonomous negotiation act of the transaction: the gateway-signed notice (agreed_by_agent or purchase_offered_by_agent) and the receipt of its delivery to the principal by the mandate's notice_route, as {code, render, content_hash, delivery}. A notice is not a step-up and has no event. A reviewer answers "I never agreed" from its content_hash.
    ts_render_digests[] Only in a bundle released to a seller or a payer PSP: for each summary step-up over a multi-seller basket, {step_up_id, render_hash, cover}, where render_hash is the sha-256 of its ts_render and cover is the covers[] entry of this transaction's leg. The other sellers' lines stay out of the bundle.
    verification The gateway's own result: steps_passed (0 to 6), chain_valid, and for step 6 catalogue_result and catalogue_unchecked[].
    bundle_signature The operator's signature over the whole bundle minus bundle_signature.

    A human-readable rendering of a bundle, the informative summary profile in.bacp.evidence.summary.1, is described in Human participants. It is built only from the signed artefacts in the bundle and is never itself evidence.

    For a negotiated purchase the bundle also carries the negotiation transcript, the accepted offer, the within_bounds attestation and the bound on_init, so that a reviewer can check what was offered and agreed without seeing the principal's reservation price (Negotiation).

    AP2 five-step verification¶

    A bundle verifies only if all six steps pass (the five AP2 steps and step 6), the InstructionLog chain is valid, and the bundle signature verifies:

    1. Closed Checkout Mandate. It is signed by the open Checkout Mandate's cnf key (HNP) or by the principal who signed the parent (HP); its parent_mandate_id is the open mandate; and the open mandate's principal and MM signatures verify.
    2. Checkout hash. The BPP signature on the quote verifies against a registry key of role BPP; sha-256(JCS(quote)) equals checkout_hash; and the closed Checkout Mandate carries that checkout_hash and the quote total.
    3. Checkout receipt. The BPP-signed checkout receipt is a success and its reference equals the hash of the closed Checkout Mandate.
    4. Closed Payment Mandate. It is signed by the open Payment Mandate's cnf key (or the principal); its parent is the open Payment Mandate, whose signatures verify; its checkout_hash and amount match; and its checkout_mandate_ref is the closed Checkout Mandate.
    5. Payment receipt. The PSP-signed payment receipt is a success; its reference equals the hash of the closed Payment Mandate; its checkout_hash and amount match; and it carries the rail evidence required for its handler (Rail evidence).
    6. Trusted Surface events. Every trusted_surface_events[] entry verifies against a registry key of role Mandate Manager or PSP. The signer of every step_up.* and negotiation.step_up.* event MUST also be one of the keys that Step-up signers derives from the mandates in the bundle: the issuer of the mandate that the render's mandate_id names, or the PSP that the table names for that step-up from the bundle's Payment Mandates. A registry Mandate Manager or PSP key that is not one of them is evidence_unverifiable. Every InstructionLog entry at stage step_up that records an approval or a decline has a matching event, whose hash the entry's content_hash carries. Every step_up.approved, mandate.activated, loan.accepted and payment.afa_completed event carries a Presentation, and for a step-up its content_hash is the hash of the step-up's ts_render and its checkout_hash is the bundle's checkout_hash. A contested crossing records late_receipt_party in its transcript entry; two receipts at one log_seq that name different entries, or a decrease of received_at along a chain without clock_step_observed, is evidence_unverifiable. A negotiation.step_up.approved event carries a Presentation with offer_hash instead of checkout_hash: the verifier MUST check that it equals the offer_hash of the step-up's ts_render and of the signed offer in the bundle's negotiation transcript, and that the ts_render's acceptance_revocation, where present, equals that offer's value; the quote comparisons below do not apply to such an event. negotiation.step_up.approved and negotiation.step_up.declined are step-up events for the next rule: their ts_render MUST be in ts_renders[], with its hash and signature checked, and a missing one is evidence_unverifiable; the quote comparisons below never apply to them. A negotiation.agreed.declined or negotiation.purchase_offer.withdrawn event carries a Presentation with offer_hash, which the verifier checks against the offer in the negotiation transcript. For every other step-up event, ts_renders[] MUST hold a ts_render whose sha-256 is that content_hash (where the event carries one) and whose signature verifies against the gateway's registry key, and the verifier MUST compare its total, its payee (seller_name) and its checkout_hash with the BPP-signed quote in checkout, and its price_breakup_hash with checkout.disclosures_hash; a missing render or any mismatch fails the step. Where the step-up's ts_render carries covers[] (a summary step-up over a basket), the verifier instead finds the covers[] entry whose session_id is the bundle's session and compares its checkout_hash and amount with the BPP-signed quote and its payee.vpa with the payee VPA of the PSP payment receipt; the summary's total, seller_name and checkout_hash, and the Presentation.checkout_hash equality above, are not checked against this leg's quote. In a bundle that carries the summary only as a ts_render_digests[] entry, the event's content_hash MUST equal its render_hash and the same comparison is made with its cover; that the cover is an entry of the render is checked by a reviewer that holds the full render (the principal, the grievance officer or the ODR body). The InstructionLog's first entry whose actor is agent is at stage ai_disclosure. Where the bundle carries checkout.bpp_signed_payment_terms, its authorization MUST verify over its body against the registry key of the BPP that the body's context.bpp_id names, as of the signature's created time, and the payee VPA of the PSP payment receipt MUST equal the payee VPA in those terms; for a Reserve Pay payment the terms MUST be present and the open Payment Mandate's payee.vpa and payee.bpp_id MUST equal the VPA and the bpp_id in them (Sibling Payment Mandates). For a session step-up of kind confirm or rail_auth the verifier also checks the catalogue disclosure: every Disclosures.catalogue[] entry of checkout.disclosures that is sponsored or undeclared, or has a prior_price, MUST have an entry in the ts_render's catalogue_disclosure[], so that an omission fails; where the session's agent-written discover InstructionLog entry exists for the bundle's transaction_id, checkout.disclosures.catalogue[] MUST hold exactly one entry for each line item of the BPP-signed quote whose item appears in a source named in that entry's refs.source_digests; each entry's sponsorship, prior_price and unit_price MUST equal (JCS) those of the Disclosures.catalogue[] entry that its index names (unit_price against discovered_unit_price; on a summary step-up, of the leg that covers[cover_index] names, which only a verifier that holds that leg's bundle can check); and counterparty_text[name_ref].text MUST equal a language value of the item's name in the BPP-signed quote for that item. For each Disclosures.catalogue[] entry whose source_digest appears in refs.source_digests of the session's discover InstructionLog entry written by the agent, the signed source in discovery_sources[] whose sha-256 is that digest MUST verify against the BPP's registry key as of the signature's created time; its context.message_id MUST equal the derivation from the idempotency_key in that log entry; and its item values MUST match those of the Disclosures.catalogue[] entry, derived by the normative mapping. An entry whose digest is not there was not checked by the agent; the verifier records it as unchecked, and it does not fail the step. Where the session's agent-written discover InstructionLog entry exists, discovery_response MUST be present, its signature MUST verify against the gateway's profile key, and the sha-256 of its body MUST equal that entry's content_hash; each entry's sponsorship.status MUST then be no weaker than, and its sponsorship.by[] a superset of, those of the same item in discovery_response. Where there is no such entry, the sponsorship comparison is recorded as unchecked and does not fail the step. This makes the verifier, not the gateway, the judge of whether the sponsorship and prior price shown at checkout are those the seller sent. Any other failure of step 6 is evidence_unverifiable; a catalogue check that fails or is unchecked is recorded in verification.catalogue_result and catalogue_unchecked[] and does not stop the issue (see Attaching evidence to an IGM issue). Step 6 proves what the principal was shown and, through EvidenceBundle.checkout.bpp_signed_payment_terms, whom the seller asked to be paid; neither the quote nor ts_render carries the payee's VPA or settlement account.

    The gateway runs this verification when it builds the bundle and records the result in verification. A reviewer SHOULD re-run it independently rather than trust verification.

    Attaching evidence to an IGM issue¶

    • Before raising an issue the gateway MUST assemble the bundle and verify it fully. If it does not pass all six verification steps with a valid chain, POST /issues returns 422 evidence_unverifiable and nothing is sent (Orders and Post-order). The one exception is the catalogue checks of step 6: a catalogue entry that fails or is unchecked does not stop the issue; the gateway records it in verification (catalogue_result, catalogue_unchecked[]) and sends the issue. A gateway MUST NOT refuse to raise an issue because of a catalogue check.
    • The Beckn issue carries the bundle by reference: evidence_bundle_ref (the /evidence/{transaction_id} URI) and evidence_bundle_hash (sha-256 over the JCS form of the bundle). The bundle itself is also returned on the Issue to the agent.
    • When the issue is escalated to GRIEVANCE, the same bundle, identified by the same hash, goes to the GRO. ODR bodies receive it the same way, out of protocol.
    • For a payment issue, the RRN or UTR in the bundle and on the issue is the join key with the rail's dispute channel.

    Access¶

    The bundle contains personal data and the principal's instructions. The gateway MUST release it only to:

    • the principal, or the agent acting under the principal's mandate for that transaction;
    • the counterparties of record (the seller and the payer PSP of that transaction);
    • the grievance redressal officer or ODR body handling an issue on it;
    • a regulator, on lawful request.

    Anyone else gets 403 evidence_forbidden. Selective disclosure keeps the basket away from the PSP and the instrument away from the seller where the format allows it. A summary step-up's full ts_render, which lists every seller of a basket, goes only to the principal (or the agent acting for the principal), the grievance officer, the ODR body and a regulator; a seller or a payer PSP gets its ts_render_digests[] entry instead.

    Retention¶

    Record Kept by Retention
    Mandates, notices, consent and sharing records Mandate Manager At least 7 years, exportable in machine-readable form
    Personal data received by a seller Each recipient Data Fiduciary dpdp.data_retention of the mandate and retention.recipient_max of the ConsentReceipt, never below the one-year minimum of DPDP Rule 8(3), and erased afterwards
    Idempotency keys and stored responses Gateway At least 24 hours (48 hours recommended)
    Replay-cache nonces Gateway Until the signature's expires (at most 300 seconds)
    InstructionLog, receipts and evidence bundles Agent operator (gateway) and PSP Long enough to serve grievances, disputes and regulatory review; see below
    BPP-signed on_search callbacks behind a returned source_digest Gateway As long as the evidence bundle of any session that used an item from them, and in any case as long as the InstructionLog
    Negotiation transcripts Agent operator Proposed: 2 years for B2C, longer for B2B and public procurement (Q-56)

    Where these records contain payment data, RBI's directive on Storage of Payment System Data (RBI/2017-18/153, 06-04-2018) already requires RBI-authorised payment system providers to store that data only in India (the foreign leg of a cross-border transaction may also be stored abroad).

    Until Q-15 is settled, a gateway, Mandate Manager or PSP adapter MUST store InstructionLogs, receipts and evidence bundles that contain payment data only in India, and MUST NOT send that payment data to any processor outside India (see Data localisation).

    Proposed

    A single retention table for InstructionLogs, receipts and evidence bundles, with downstream erasure on a deadline, is open (Q-17).
