# Regulatory Mapping

Version 2026-10-18

This page is part of BACP version `2026-10-18`. Rules marked **Proposed** depend on an open question and may change in a later dated release.

This page maps every regulatory and policy requirement that BACP tracks to the part of the specification that answers it. Each row gives the requirement, the instrument it comes from, the BACP control, and whether BACP `2026-10-18` **covers** it, covers it **partially**, or leaves a **gap**. Every partial or gap row names what is missing and, where one exists, links the open question that must be answered first.

This is the only page that uses requirement identifiers (`REQ-…`, `PAY-…`, `NEG-…`, `TRADE-…`). They are stable labels for traceability between this page, the open questions and conformance reports.

Not legal advice

This mapping is an engineering aid for implementers and reviewers. **It is not legal advice** and it does not establish that any deployment complies with any law, direction or network policy. Several sources are cited from secondary material or are unverified, and are marked as such. Laws, directions and circulars change; the citations were last checked as of 17-10-2026. Each participant remains responsible for its own compliance and should take advice from qualified counsel.

## How to read this page

- **Status judges the specification, not a deployment.** "Covered" means the specification as written satisfies the requirement. It does not mean any implementation has been certified.
- **Group** is the regulator or body whose instrument is the primary source. A row is listed once, under its primary source, even when several instruments apply.
- **Forward-dated duties.** DPDP duties that start in November 2026 or May 2027 are mapped now; their dates are in [Security and Privacy](https://spec.bacp.dev/draft/specification/security-and-privacy/#commencement).
- **N/A** rows are operator duties outside the wire protocol (contracts, audits, data-lifecycle jobs), which belong in an operator conformance checklist, or requirements whose source has been withdrawn.
- **Source labels.** A source with no qualifier was checked against the primary text (RBI, NPCI, MeitY, Gazette, ONDC or statute). "Secondary", "press", "not verified" or "not checked" marks a source that could not be seen in a primary text. "BACP policy" marks the part of a requirement that BACP adds beyond what the instrument says.
