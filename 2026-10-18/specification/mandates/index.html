    Mandates and Consent¶

    Version 2026-10-18

    This page is part of BACP version 2026-10-18. Rules marked Proposed depend on an open question and may change in a later dated release.

    A mandate is the signed, immutable statement of what a principal (the person or business on whose behalf an agent acts) has authorised the agent to do. Every BACP commitment that moves money, shares personal data or binds the principal is evaluated against a mandate by deterministic, non-LLM code at the gateway. A ConsentReceipt is the DPDP record of the notice the principal saw and the personal data they agreed to release to a named recipient.

    Artefact Schema Signed by
    Mandate (bacp.AgentMandate) mandate.json Principal and Mandate Manager (open); agent cnf key or principal (closed)
    ConsentReceipt (DPDP) consent-receipt.json Mandate Manager

    Negotiation bounds (reservation price, walk-away terms) are not carried by this mandate. They live in a separate negotiation mandate, described in Negotiation, so that verifiers that only understand checkout and payment mandates never meet a constraint they do not know.

    Mandate kinds and forms¶

    One schema covers four mandate types: the AP2 matrix of open/closed × Checkout/Payment. The vct and the wire-compatible ap2_vct name the type.

    Checkout (kind: checkout) Payment (kind: payment)
    Open (form: open) in.bacp.mandate.checkout.open.1: what the agent may buy, where, how much, with what data in.bacp.mandate.payment.open.1: the standing payment authority, bound to a rail primitive through rail_binding
    Closed (form: closed) in.bacp.mandate.checkout.1: one exact basket (checkout_hash) and amount in.bacp.mandate.payment.1: one exact payment, authorised through auth_binding
    • An open mandate is long-lived. It carries constraints and the agent key (cnf) that is allowed to close it. It is created only through the Mandate Manager and the Trusted Surface (Issuance).
    • A closed mandate narrows one open mandate to a single transaction. It names its parent in parent_mandate_id and carries checkout_hash and amount; the schema requires all three for form: closed.
    • The closed Checkout Mandate goes to the seller (BPP). The closed Payment Mandate goes to the payer PSP. The PSP never sees the basket and the seller never sees the instrument: the payment leg carries only payee, amount, mandate reference and checkout_hash.
    • Every Payment Mandate, open or closed, MUST carry checkout_mandate_ref, the Checkout Mandate it pays for.
    • An open Payment Mandate MUST carry rail_binding. A closed Payment Mandate MUST carry auth_binding and MUST NOT carry rail_binding.

    Required content of an open mandate¶

    Besides the fields the schema requires of every mandate (vct, mandate_id, kind, form, iss, iat, valid_from, valid_until, principal, agent, purpose, action_scope, presence_allowed, constraints, revocation, signatures), an open mandate MUST carry:

    • cnf, the agent key that may close it;
    • data_scopes (it MAY be empty);
    • audit_uri, where every use is reported;
    • dpdp.data_retention, the ISO 8601 retention period imposed on recipients;
    • at least one frequency bound: constraints.budgets or constraints.max_executions.

    A closed mandate inherits these from its parent and need not repeat them.

    Immutability and delegation¶

    Everything in a mandate except status, usage and signatures is signed and immutable. The mandate hash, used as Receipt.reference (see Evidence), is sha-256 over the JCS (RFC 8785) form of the mandate minus those three fields. An edit to any signed field, such as a raised cap, makes every signature fail, and the gateway returns 403 mandate_invalid_signature.

    parent_mandate_id chains (a closed mandate, or a sub-agent delegation requested with parent_mandate_id on POST /mandates) MUST only narrow: the child's scope, constraints and validity are each a subset of the parent's. A closed mandate's valid_until MUST NOT be later than its parent's.

    Issuance¶

    An agent never signs or activates an open mandate. It requests one. The principal approves it on a Trusted Surface (the payer PSP's app, or a Mandate Manager app), and the Mandate Manager (MM) issues it.

    1. The agent calls POST /mandates (tag bacp-browse) with a MandateRequest: purpose_code, rail, presence_allowed, proposed_constraints, data_scopes, action_scope, validity (an ISO 8601 duration), language, channel (the channel on which the principal will approve) and webhook_url, and REQUIRED operator_compensation (Operator compensation). The gateway MUST accept webhook_url, so that mandate.status_changed can reach the agent before any session exists. To add a seller to an existing root open Checkout Mandate, the request also carries checkout_mandate_ref (pattern ^mdt_[A-Za-z0-9]{12,40}$). A request for a payee-bound primitive carries payee{vpa, bpp_id}, and a request for a Reserve Pay block also carries the block amount it asks for in rail_limits.block_amount (Sibling Payment Mandates). A request that the principal originates on the Trusted Surface to move a mandate to another gateway carries supersedes{mandate_id} (Porting a mandate); an agent never sends it. Proposed: when action_scope is omitted, all eight actions (DISCOVER, QUOTE, ORDER, PAY, CANCEL, RETURN, RAISE_ISSUE, SHARE_DATA) are requested, and the Trusted Surface shows that full scope to the principal before approval.
    2. Before anything reaches the Trusted Surface, the gateway or the MM MUST reject the request:
      • with 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible if rail resolves to a handler that cannot back an open mandate at this gateway: one that is not open-eligible or has no binding.open_primitives, a reserved or deprecated descriptor, or a pilot descriptor that the gateway's conformance attestation does not list (Handler binding);
      • with 403 constraint_unknown if proposed_constraints has a key this page does not define (see Fail-closed handling);
      • with 400 invalid_request at $.operator_compensation if the request has no operator_compensation, or one that is not consistent with the disclosure.compensation_model of the signing AgentCredential (Operator compensation);
      • with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch if it has no amount_per_txn; has no periodic budget; has no categories_allow, or one outside the purpose's categories; asks for a longer validity than the purpose allows; exceeds any per-transaction or per-period bound of the purpose (Purpose bounds); asks for a data scope the purpose does not allow; names a purpose_code that is not in the purpose-code registry; or exceeds the static limits of the requested rail, including a rail_limits.block_amount above the handler's per-block limit in the handler registry (at $.rail_limits.block_amount);
      • if rail resolves to in.upi.reservepay (primitive sbmd_block): with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.payee if the request has no payee, and at $.rail_limits.block_amount if it has no rail_limits.block_amount; with 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible if the gateway holds no BPP-signed payment terms of the seller payee.bpp_id and does not obtain them first (a Beckn init to that seller, never followed by confirm), if that payee is the buyer app (a block collected by the BAP) and presence_allowed includes HNP, or if payee.vpa is not the VPA in those terms; and, for every Reserve Pay request, with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch if the requested rail_limits.block_amount breaks the sum-of-blocks rule, or 403 mandate_exhausted if it does not fit the root's headroom, at $.checkout_mandate_ref for a request with checkout_mandate_ref and at $.rail_limits.block_amount for a request without it, whose root is the one it proposes. These checks run before anything reaches the rail, so the principal's PSP is never asked to create a block that the MM would then refuse.
    3. Otherwise the gateway returns 202 with a MandateHandle in PENDING, carrying a trusted_surface_url deep link, which is an https URL on an origin of the issuing Mandate Manager or a rail deep link that opens the payer PSP's own app (Origins and delivery). The link expires after at most 15 minutes.
    4. The principal opens the link. The Trusted Surface shows the notice in the principal's language (English or a language of the Eighth Schedule): limits, categories, data items, recipient class, withdrawal route and grievance route, and the operator compensation: what the principal pays the operator, and what sellers pay the operator or the buyer app. The notice, with the compensation, is hashed into the ConsentReceipt notice. For a payee-bound primitive the notice also shows the payee name the bank returns for payee.vpa and the seller's bpp_id. For a Reserve Pay block it shows the requested block amount and, unless the payer PSP attests a block-debit feed, the unseen-draw statement (Usage ledger). For a block whose attested funding is credit, the notice says "credit" in the principal's language and, for an interest-bearing credit line, that interest may run from each debit (NPCI UPI Consolidated Circular v1.0, para 26.1.2). For a sibling Payment Mandate it shows the other Payment Mandates under the same root, their combined total, and the amount each budget of the root has left (its max less the root's exposure). The principal approves with an explicit action and the rail's own authentication (for UPI, the UPI PIN).
    5. The payer PSP creates or links the rail primitive (for example a Reserve Pay block, a UPI Circle delegation or an AutoPay mandate). It MUST use a primitive listed in the handler descriptor's binding.open_primitives, and signs a rail attestation: a JWS over {rail_ref, type, primitive, limits, expiry, payee, funding} (and, for a gift PPI, custody). It MUST NOT create a Reserve Pay block larger than the requested rail_limits.block_amount.
    6. The MM checks handler eligibility against the current registry, taken from the dated BACP release and never from a gateway endpoint, records that registry's version in rail_binding.registry_version (and, where it reads the release manifest, the registry_serial and the digest of the registry file in rail_binding.registry_serial and rail_binding.registry_digest), and MUST refuse issuance (rail_binding_not_open_eligible) if the attested primitive is not in the descriptor's binding.open_primitives, the descriptor is not open-eligible, or its status is neither live nor an attested pilot. For a pilot handler the MM MUST first verify the gateway's conformance attestation, forwarded in the request's gateway_attestation, find the handler in its pilots[], and write rail_binding.conformance{iss, sub, jti} into the mandate. The MM then copies agent.bap_id from the verified AgentCredential's bap.subscriber_id into the mandate, never from the request, and checks that every BACP constraint is at or below the attested rail limits, and, for a Reserve Pay block, that the attested block_amount is at most the requested one and that the attested expiry is no later than the root open Checkout Mandate's valid_until. It refuses an attested funding of credit (or a missing one, or an overdraft account without a deposit attestation) when the root lacks credit_funding_allowed: true, with 403 credit_funding_not_allowed (Handler binding), and refuses a gift PPI whose attestation does not state custody: principal_side. It builds the open Checkout and open Payment Mandates, or, for a request with checkout_mandate_ref, only the open Payment Mandate (Sibling Payment Mandates). If the MM refuses issuance at this step after the rail primitive exists, it MUST revoke the primitive at the rail and report the outcome in rail_revocation{status} on the mandate.rejected event it sends to the gateway. A refused block whose revocation is unconfirmed is exposure at its attested block_amount until the revocation is confirmed or the block expires, although it never became ACTIVE (Usage ledger). The principal signs both (signatures[].role: principal) and the MM countersigns both (role: mandate_manager). The MM allocates a status-list entry per mandate and issues the ConsentReceipt.
    7. The MM sends a signed mandate.activated event to the gateway's /trusted-surface/events (tag bacp-ts, registry key of role MM or PSP). The event MUST carry the Presentation of the approval: surface, channel, language, modality, the hash of the notice shown and the principal's action. The gateway records an InstructionLog entry at stage mandate and sends the webhook mandate.status_changed (ACTIVE) to the agent. For a sibling Reserve Pay block the gateway applies the activation inside the atomic section keyed on the root open Checkout Mandate (Usage ledger), so that an activation and a complete never both take the same headroom: if the block at its full attested amount no longer fits every budget window that contains the activation, the gateway refuses the event with 403 mandate_exhausted and never treats the mandate as ACTIVE; the MM then revokes the mandate and its block at the rail and sends mandate.revoked with rail_revocation{status}. If that status is unconfirmed, the block is exposure at its attested block_amount until the revocation is confirmed or the block expires.

    A verifier MUST accept an open mandate only if a principal signature and an MM signature both verify against registry-resolvable keys of those roles, and the rail attestation verifies against a key of role PSP and matches rail_binding.rail_ref, rail_binding.type and rail_binding.primitive.

    Operator compensation¶

    A principal who knows how the operator behind her agent is paid can discount its advice. The mandate request therefore carries operator_compensation, and the Trusted Surface shows it in the notice before the principal approves.

    {
  "from_principal": [{"basis": "per_order", "amount": {"value": 2000, "currency": "INR"}}],
  "from_sellers": "finder_fee"
}
    Field Content
    from_principal[] What the principal pays: one or more {basis, amount?}. basis is per_order (a fee on each order), period (a fee for a period named in the operator's terms) or none. amount is REQUIRED for per_order and period, and absent for none. A none entry stands alone.
    from_sellers What sellers pay the operator or the buyer app for orders it brings: finder_fee (an ONDC buyer finder fee), channel_margin, none or other.
    • The Mandate Manager MUST NOT activate a mandate whose request has no operator_compensation (400 invalid_request at $.operator_compensation; the mandate never becomes ACTIVE), and MUST show the value in the notice and hash it into the ConsentReceipt notice (notice.operator_compensation is REQUIRED).
    • The source of the declaration is the operator. When the AgentCredential carries disclosure.compensation_model, the request MUST be consistent with it: none means every from_principal basis is none and from_sellers is none; seller_paid means from_principal is none and from_sellers is not none; principal_paid means a from_principal entry is not none and from_sellers is none; mixed means both. Otherwise the request is refused with 400 invalid_request at $.operator_compensation.
    • This is BACP policy, not a statutory duty. No Indian rule found requires an agent operator to disclose a seller-paid commission (not verified as of 18-10-2026), and BACP cites no law for it (Q-79).
    • When a session's intermediary_compensation[] has an entry with source: seller while the mandate's notice says from_sellers: none, the gateway adds the warning operator_compensation_mismatch (requires_buyer_review) and logs it at stage disclosure. It tells the principal once per mandate on the Trusted Surface and reports the mismatch to the Mandate Manager. The Mandate Manager then asks the operator for a corrected notice, which takes effect only on the principal's re-approval.
    • When the AgentCredential has no compensation_model, the notice says that the operator has not declared its compensation model to its credential issuer.
    • Per-order amounts are shown again at the time of the order as an operator charge. What sellers pay is carried per order in intermediary_compensation[] and is never read out per order.

    Sibling Payment Mandates¶

    A Reserve Pay block pays one seller, so a principal who shops hands-free across several sellers holds one open Payment Mandate per payee-bound instrument, all under one root open Checkout Mandate whose budgets cap the total (Multi-seller basket).

    • Requesting a sibling. A MandateRequest with checkout_mandate_ref asks for one more open Payment Mandate under an existing root. checkout_mandate_ref MUST name an ACTIVE root open Checkout Mandate of the same principal; otherwise the MM refuses it with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.checkout_mandate_ref. The MM builds only an open Payment Mandate, whose checkout_mandate_ref is that root and whose constraints narrow the root's (Immutability and delegation). It never builds a second Checkout Mandate or a second budget for the same basket. parent_mandate_id stays reserved for sub-agent delegation.
    • Payee. An open Payment Mandate whose rail_binding.primitive is sbmd_block MUST carry payee{vpa, bpp_id} and, when the seller's BPP-signed terms name a provider, payee.provider_id, which MUST equal message.order.provider.id of those terms at every leg. A leg whose provider differs is refused with 403 reservepay_block_wrong_payee and nothing is debited (a BACP rule whose basis is RBI Master Direction on Payment Aggregators 2025 para 13(f), under which a PA ensures that a marketplace takes no payment for a seller not onboarded on it, and para 13(e), a merchant ID per merchant; the block-payee match itself is BACP policy); one seller app can serve several shops from one collection VPA, which is why the VPA alone is not enough (whether ONDC seller apps share one VPA is not verified as of 18-10-2026). A request without it is refused with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.payee. payee.vpa MUST equal the VPA in the seller's BPP-signed payment terms (Beckn payments[].params.virtual_payment_address of on_init), and the attested payee MUST equal payee.vpa; otherwise issuance is refused with 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible and nothing is sent to the rail (Handler binding).
    • The payee is bound to BPP-signed terms, always. The gateway MUST hold the BPP-signed payment terms of the seller payee.bpp_id before a Reserve Pay request goes to the Trusted Surface, including for a set-up outside any checkout. If it holds none, it obtains them first by a Beckn init to that seller that it never follows with confirm, or refuses the request with 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible. The terms are the raw on_init body as received together with its Beckn Authorization header, verified against the registry key of the BPP named in context.bpp_id, which MUST be payee.bpp_id. No per-object JWS is needed (Q-43; Beckn signing). The gateway keeps them as bpp_signed_payment_terms for every Reserve Pay leg (Evidence bundle).
    • Requested block amount. A Reserve Pay request carries rail_limits.block_amount, the block the principal is asked to set up. The gateway and the MM check it at step 2 of Issuance, the notice shows it, and the payer PSP creates no larger block. It MUST NOT exceed the handler's per-block limit in the handler registry; otherwise the request is refused at step 2 with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.rail_limits.block_amount. If the attested block_amount is larger than the requested one, the MM refuses issuance at step 6 and revokes the block at the rail.
    • Every Reserve Pay request is checked against a root. The per-block limit, the sum-of-blocks rule and the headroom check below apply to every Reserve Pay request, with or without checkout_mandate_ref. A request without it proposes a new root: the checks then use the request's own proposed_constraints.budgets as the root's budgets, the exposure that already counts against a new root of the same principal (unconfirmed blocks) as the root's exposure, and $.rail_limits.block_amount instead of $.checkout_mandate_ref as the error location.
    • Sum of blocks. The block_amounts of all Reserve Pay mandates under one root, including the one requested (its requested amount at step 2, its attested amount at step 6), MUST NOT exceed the smallest budgets[].max of the root; and, with the requested block at its full amount, the root's exposure (Usage ledger) MUST fit every budget window that contains the activation. A request that breaks the first rule is refused with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.checkout_mandate_ref, and one that breaks the second (no headroom) with 403 mandate_exhausted at $.checkout_mandate_ref; the first rule is checked before the second. The gateway checks the headroom at step 2 and again, authoritatively, when it applies the activation inside the root's atomic section (step 7), so that an issuance never races a complete for the same headroom.
    • Block expiry within the root. The attested expiry of a block MUST NOT be later than the root open Checkout Mandate's valid_until; otherwise the MM refuses issuance at step 6 and revokes the block.
    • One root per principal. The parallel-root key is the principal's account at the Mandate Manager: the one account behind every pairwise principal.ref that the MM issues for that person, whichever agent or operator asked. The MM MUST refuse a new root open Checkout Mandate for a principal who already holds one or more ACTIVE roots at that MM, with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.checkout_mandate_ref (use checkout_mandate_ref to add a seller to an existing root), unless the notice the principal approves for it states the budgets of every other ACTIVE root and the combined total of all of them. A root at another Mandate Manager is outside this rule: each open mandate belongs to one gateway (agent.bap_id), and a move to another gateway is a successor root (Porting a mandate).
    • Unconfirmed blocks outlive their root. A block whose rail revocation is still unconfirmed when its root ends counts as exposure against every new root of the same principal (by the key above) until the MM reports it confirmed or the block expires. A PSP that never confirms can therefore hold at most the block's amount for at most the block's validity (at most 90 days, Rail Bindings). The Trusted Surface shows the principal the route to revoke the block in the UPI app (NPCI UPI Consolidated Circular v1.0, para 16.10).
    • Set-up outside a checkout. A principal may set up a block for a seller in advance, outside any checkout, through this request. The rule that a new block is offered only beside a one-time human-present payment (Per-leg rule, 3b) does not apply to such a set-up.

    Porting a mandate to another gateway¶

    A principal who changes buyer app or gateway does not move an open mandate: the mandate is bound to one gateway (agent.bap_id), and a successor root replaces it.

    • Principal-initiated, on the Trusted Surface only. The principal approves the successor on the Trusted Surface, on the same terms as any issuance (Issuance). The successor carries supersedes{mandate_id}, and the MM revokes the superseded root, with its siblings and their blocks as in Revocation, before the successor becomes ACTIVE. A MandateRequest that an agent or a gateway submits with supersedes is refused with 403 trusted_surface_only. A support operator or an assisted operator never ports a mandate for a principal.
    • No prompt and no cap. A gateway MUST NOT prompt or induce a port, for example by lowering a limit, adding a friction or offering a reward for it. BACP sets no numeric cap on ports: the UPI AutoPay rule that a port can be done three times in a rolling 90-day period binds UPI members, not BACP (NPCI UPI Consolidated Circular v1.0, para 14.24.4).
    • The carried spend. The MM signs the spend that the principal has already made into the successor as carried_exposure[]{period, window_start, amount, source}, derived from its own use events (Mandate events). The successor's windows start at that spend, so a port never restores budget. No statement of the gateway that is being left is needed, so that gateway cannot hold the principal back by staying silent. For a window in which the MM's record has a gap, source is incomplete: the Trusted Surface says so in the principal's language and pre-fills that window at the superseded root's max. The principal may change any pre-filled figure before approving, because the cap is the principal's own, and the change is accepted only with the principal's own factor. On revocation of the superseded root its gateway MUST send, within 60 seconds, a MandateEvent of type closed with last_seq. Until the Mandate Manager holds every event up to last_seq, every window that contains the time since the last event received is incomplete. A use of the superseded root that the Mandate Manager receives after it has signed the successor counts against the successor: the Mandate Manager sends the successor's gateway a signed carried-exposure increment, which that gateway applies inside the root's atomic section (Q-104 records that the wire form of the increment is not yet defined). Until it is defined, the Mandate Manager does not sign the successor before it holds a closed event and every event up to its last_seq, or 60 seconds have passed without one. In that second case every window is incomplete and is pre-filled at max, so the carried exposure is treated as at the old max. A use that arrives after signing makes the Mandate Manager revoke the successor and ask the principal to port again.
    • Reserve Pay blocks. A successor MAY re-bind a block of its predecessor when the payer PSP signs a fresh attestation for the same block. A re-bound block is not revoked with the predecessor, its exposure moves to the successor inside the root's atomic section, and it never counts on both roots. Where the payer PSP does not re-attest, the old block is revoked and a new one is created, which asks the principal for the UPI PIN once for each seller, and both blocks count as exposure while a revocation is unconfirmed. Whether payer PSPs re-attest is open with NPCI.
    • Revocation parity. No BACP route to revoke or move a mandate needs more steps or factors than the rail's own withdrawal (Revocation).

    Confirmation floor¶

    confirmation_floor records how the principal wants approvals to be given, as one object with two optional members and at least one present. It is a constraint, because only constraints fails closed: a gateway that does not know it refuses the mandate (Fail-closed handling), so a principal who sets a floor cannot use a gateway that has not upgraded.

    • min_user_action is the weakest user_action that the principal accepts. The ladder is tap_confirm, then voice_confirm_plus_device, then upi_pin, biometric and passkey, which are equal. Voice never releases money (Voice confirmation), so the ladder bites on non-money confirm and post_order approvals.
    • channels_allow lists the channels on which the principal accepts an approval (at least one, no repeats). A request such as "always call me, never a tap" can be read aloud in the principal's language.
    • Set only by the principal. The floor is set or changed only by the principal on the Trusted Surface at issuance, never through MandateRequest (a request that carries it is refused), so that an agent, including a grandson's agent acting for a grandmother, cannot lower it. The activation notice reads it in full in the principal's language.
    • Absent means any. A mandate without it accepts any user action on any channel, so that a feature-phone principal on IVR keeps working.
    • Enforcement. The gateway refuses an approval below the floor, or outside the channels, with 403 presence_not_proven at $.presentation.user_action or $.presentation.channel, and it never offers a delivery outside the channels. If the only allowed channel is unavailable it answers 503 trusted_surface_unavailable with reason: channel_not_allowed and the session's handoff routes.
    • It is the principal's standing preference, which TS-3 alone does not record. It is not a security closure.

    Budgets¶

    Each budgets[] entry names a period and a max. A mandate MAY carry several, and every one applies.

    Period Window at evaluation time t
    P1D Rolling: payments at times in (t − 24 h, t].
    P7D Rolling: payments at times in (t − 7 days, t].
    P1M The calendar month in IST (Asia/Kolkata) that contains t. It resets at 00:00 IST on the first of the month.
    FIRST_24H [valid_from, valid_from + 24 h). After this window the budget no longer applies.
    LIFETIME Every payment over the life of the mandate.

    Rail-side "per month" caps, such as the Circle monthly cap and the AFA period, are also evaluated per calendar month in IST.

    The check is exposure(window) + amount ≤ max, where exposure is settled spend plus in-flight reservations in that window (Usage ledger). A breach returns 403 mandate_exhausted with resolution: requires_buyer_input and moves no money.

    Fail-closed handling of unknown constraints¶

    The set of constraint keys in the table above, and the set of step_up keys, is closed.

    • A gateway, MM or verifier that meets a constraint key or step_up key it does not know MUST refuse the mandate with 403 constraint_unknown (unrecoverable). It MUST NOT ignore the key, and MUST NOT evaluate the remaining keys and proceed.
    • This applies at POST /mandates (to proposed_constraints) and at every evaluation.
    • This is the one exception to BACP's rule that readers ignore unknown fields. New constraint types arrive as new mandate types (as the negotiation mandate does), never as new keys that an older verifier would skip.
    • Any other malformed mandate fails closed: 400 mandate_schema_invalid for a schema failure, 403 mandate_invalid for unparseable or inconsistent content.

    Presence (HP and HNP)¶

    presence is HP (human present) or HNP (human not present). It is declared on every session and every post-order write, and travels on the network in context.bacp.presence.

    • The presence of a request MUST be in the open mandate's presence_allowed. Otherwise the gateway returns 403 presence_not_allowed (requires_buyer_review).
    • HNP. The agent closes the open mandates itself with its cnf key (Closing). The closed Payment Mandate's auth_binding.type is parent_rail_binding: the debit runs under the open mandate's rail binding. Only class-O handlers may back an HNP payment (Handler binding).
    • HP. The principal signs the closed mandates on the Trusted Surface and authorises the payment with a fresh per-transaction factor (auth_binding.type is upi.pin, upi.biometric, card.3ds or instrument). A one-off UPI payment authorised by PIN is identified as handler in.upi.intent with auth_binding.type: upi.pin. An HP payment does not draw on the open mandate's rail binding.
    • An HP step-up payment counts toward usage.count but not toward the open mandate's budgets.
    • The basis of a debit that takes no new factor. A closed Payment Mandate that executes under the parent's rail binding (HNP) carries, in the record that the gateway stores, auth_binding.afa_ref{handler, primitive, registry_version}: a reference to the registry descriptor at that version, not a copy. Only the gateway sets it, and an agent that supplies it gets 400 invalid_request. The principal's post-debit notice and the evidence summary render the basis from the descriptor through a fixed template, so that support staff need no registry lookup. For the hi-IN notice of a recurring AutoPay debit the sentence is "यह पैसा उस ऑटो-पे से कटा है जिसे आपने पहले मंज़ूरी दी थी, इसलिए PIN नहीं माँगा गया।", which says in English "This debit is under the AutoPay you approved earlier, so no PIN was asked." The basis records which rule the debit relies on. It does not make the debit lawful: the issuer remains liable under RBI/2025-26/79 para 9(b) for a non-compliant debit. The descriptor's afa.rbi_annex1_item names the Annexure-1 row where the handler relies on an RBI exemption, and a handler that relies on authentication at block creation or registration (Reserve Pay, OTM, UPI Circle) has none: it is never presented as an RBI exemption (Handler registry). UPI Circle is not open-eligible while Q-77 is open, so no HNP debit runs through it.
    • Voice never releases money. The gateway refuses voice_confirm_plus_device on a rail_auth step-up and on any confirm step-up whose approval releases a debit, with 403 presence_not_proven at $.presentation.user_action (Voice confirmation).
    • Accepting a credit offer (a loan, BNPL) is always HP. An agent attempt returns 403 credit_acceptance_requires_hp.
    • Returns, and accepting or rejecting an IGM resolution, are HP (Post-order gate).
    • HP is proven, not declared. Every post-order write with presence: HP (cancel, return, raise issue, issue action, and an agent's trade act: GRN, CRAC, MSMED objection, ledger acknowledgement) MUST carry hp_proof{ts_event_hash, step_up_id}, both REQUIRED: the id of a step-up of kind post_order whose gateway-signed ts_render names this write (action, order_id and, for an issue action, issue_id and, for RESOLUTION_ACCEPTED or RESOLUTION_REJECTED, the subject_hash of the resolution shown), and the sha-256 over the JCS form of the signed step_up.approved event that the gateway received for it at /trusted-surface/events. A write without hp_proof gets 403 presence_not_proven (requires_buyer_review) carrying step_up (kind post_order, reason presence_proof) for the principal to confirm, the same pending step-up on a repeat; a proof issued for another action, order or issue, or for an issue whose resolution has changed since, gets the same code. A proof is single-use: the gateway consumes it inside the atomic section keyed on the order's root Checkout Mandate (Usage ledger). Nothing is sent to the network until the proof passes (Presence proof). An HP complete is proven by the principal's signatures on the closed mandates and needs no hp_proof.

    Closing an open mandate¶

    To pay HNP, the agent turns the open Checkout and Payment Mandates into closed ones bound to the exact quote that the gateway returned in ready_for_complete.

    The agent MUST produce:

    Field Closed Checkout Mandate Closed Payment Mandate
    vct in.bacp.mandate.checkout.1 in.bacp.mandate.payment.1
    parent_mandate_id the open Checkout Mandate the open Payment Mandate
    checkout_hash session.quote.checkout_hash the same
    amount exactly the quote total the same
    checkout_mandate_ref n/a the closed Checkout Mandate's mandate_id
    payee n/a the collecting party's VPA
    auth_binding n/a {type: parent_rail_binding, handler, ref: rail_ref, psp_id}
    Validity within the parent's, typically minutes the same

    Key binding. Each closed mandate MUST carry an inner signature with role: agent made by the open mandate's cnf key. It MUST be sent as an attached compact JWS whose payload is the mandate JSON (including that inner signature) and whose header kid is the cnf key id. The same key signs the agent's RFC 9421 requests, so a mandate closed under one agent's key cannot be presented by another (Identity and Request Signing). An HP closed mandate is instead signed, inside and outside, by the principal key that signed the parent.

    The agent MAY also send a key-binding JWT (kb_jwt) on complete. If present it MUST be signed by the cnf key with aud set to the seller's bpp_id and nonce set to the Beckn message_id of the confirm that the gateway will send. The gateway derives that message_id as UUIDv5(message_id_namespace, agent_id | idempotency_key | "confirm") and publishes message_id_namespace in /.well-known/bacp. The gateway forwards the kb_jwt to the BPP in context.bacp.mandate_ref.kb_jwt.

    Encoding

    The attached-JWS closing above is the only interoperable encoding of a closed mandate in BACP 2026-10-18, and the conformance suite will carry test vectors for it. An SD-JWT / KB-JWT selective-disclosure encoding is not part of this version; a later release may add one as a new mandate vct (settled in Q-41). Verifiers MUST support both ES256 and EdDSA (Ed25519) signatures (settled in Q-42; see Identity and Request Signing).

    A closed Payment Mandate is single use. The gateway and the PSP adapter MUST allow at most one debit per closed Payment Mandate mandate_id and at most one per checkout_hash, except that under a payment plan each tender has its own closed Payment Mandate and is debited at most once, and the debits of all the tenders of one live plan together never exceed all_in_total. Presenting a used one returns 403 mandate_scope_mismatch. A closed Payment Mandate that carries tender is for one tender of one plan and MUST NOT pay a complete that has no payment_plan.

    Handler binding of open mandates¶

    An open Payment Mandate's rail_binding.type names the payment handler that backs it, and rail_binding.primitive names the mandate primitive of that handler that carries the standing authority (one of its mandate_primitives[].type). Handlers are grouped into binding classes in the Handler Registry.

    • rail_binding.type MUST validate against the class-O id pattern (RailTypeV1) or a legacy alias of one. Ids of class C, A, D or N handlers fail schema validation and are refused.
    • Rail- or issuer-enforced primitives only. An open Payment Mandate MAY bind to any class-O handler, but only through a mandate primitive whose limits the rail or the issuer enforces (limits_enforced_by is rail or issuer). The descriptor lists those primitives in binding.open_primitives. rail_binding.primitive MUST be one of them, and the PSP attestation MUST cover it. For example, in.ppi.wallet lists only ppi_emandate, so a wallet balance (balance_cap) never backs an open mandate.
    • Funding. The PSP attestation states funding as deposit or credit. A savings or current account is deposit; a RuPay credit card or a pre-sanctioned credit line is credit; an overdraft account is credit unless the PSP attests deposit; and a missing funding is treated as credit. An attested credit without credit_funding_allowed: true on the root gets 403 credit_funding_not_allowed at issuance, where the MM sends mandate.rejected with rail_revocation, and at complete on an existing mandate. A block that is refused after the rail call counts as exposure until its revocation is confirmed (Usage ledger).
    • Gift PPI custody. in.ppi.gift is open-eligible only when the PSP attestation states custody: principal_side, that is, that the vault of the gift code is on the principal's side (the Trusted Surface or the payer PSP). With custody: gateway_side, or none, the MM refuses issuance with rail_binding_not_open_eligible, because the agent's side would hold the code. A gift PPI then remains usable human-present.
    • Schema validity is not eligibility. After alias resolution the handler's descriptor MUST have open_eligible: true, MUST list rail_binding.primitive in binding.open_primitives, and MUST have status: live, or status: pilot only where the gateway's conformance attestation lists that pilot in pilots[] and has not expired. A reserved or deprecated descriptor (for example the reserved in.upi.uap.*) MUST NOT back a mandate. A binding that fails any of these checks gets 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible.
    • Checked at issuance. The MM MUST refuse to issue an open Payment Mandate whose binding fails these checks, and MUST verify the gateway's conformance attestation before it issues one on a pilot handler (Issuance, step 6). While the conformance-authority list has no entry, no attestation verifies, so an MM refuses open mandates on every pilot handler, including in.upi.circle and in.card.agentic.
    • Registry source. The MM and the gateway MUST take the registry from the dated BACP release (its source/handlers/registry.json), and MUST NOT take it from a gateway endpoint such as GET /payment-handlers, which is unsigned (Discovery). There is no registry-signed snapshot, because no party holds a registry signing key. Each publication of the registry files, including a backport that amends a dated release in place, is described by the release manifest source/handlers/release.json (registry-release.json): registry_version, a strictly increasing registry_serial (at least 1), published_at (IST, +05:30) and files[] with the sha-256 digest of each file over its JCS form. A reader that wants to prove which copy it used compares that digest. The MM records the version it checked against in rail_binding.registry_version, and MAY also record rail_binding.registry_serial and rail_binding.registry_digest.
    • The attestation is bound into the mandate. On a pilot handler the open Payment Mandate carries rail_binding.conformance{iss, sub, jti}: the conformance authority, the gateway and the attestation's jti. The schema leaves it optional, and a conformance rule makes it REQUIRED for every pilot binding (Conformance attestation). At complete the gateway refuses the mandate when it is absent or its sub is not the gateway itself, and re-checks the gateway's current attestation (the bound jti, or after a lift the newer attestation that lifted the pause).
    • Re-checked at every complete. Eligibility (status, attestation and primitive) is not fixed at issuance. The gateway MUST re-check it at step 1 of Deterministic evaluation on every complete, against the current registry, never against a copy taken when the mandate was issued.
    • 24-hour freshness. A gateway MUST adopt a status downgrade within 24 hours of the published_at of the publication that makes it. It MUST refuse an HNP complete on a handler whose descriptor copy it last refreshed from the registry source more than 24 hours earlier, or whose copy carries a lower registry_serial than a publication whose published_at is more than 24 hours past, with 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible, reasons: [registry_copy_stale] and resolution: recoverable. A stale copy is a gateway condition, not a fault of the binding: the gateway does not pause the mandate, the agent retries later without involving the principal, and an HP complete is not refused for staleness.
    • A downgrade pauses the mandate. When a registry change, or a change to the gateway's conformance attestation, makes the binding of a mandate ineligible (for example a handler moved from live to pilot without an attested pilot, or to reserved or deprecated), the gateway MUST add the pause reason binding_ineligible to every affected mandate, recording the registry_version and registry_serial of the publication that made the binding ineligible. If the mandate was ACTIVE, it becomes PAUSED and the gateway sends mandate.status_changed (PAUSED, reason: binding_ineligible, with pause_reasons and affected_sessions) to the agent and cancels every open session under the mandate with the message rail_binding_not_open_eligible. Every later complete under it gets 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible with reasons: [binding_ineligible] while the binding stays ineligible.
    • A lapsed attestation pauses the mandate. When the bound attestation is revoked, or its exp passes, or its authority's entry is removed, lapsed or revoked, or its key is removed from the entry, at a re-check, the gateway adds binding_ineligible with the cause attestation, and writes the attestation's jti into the InstructionLog entry for the addition. A status list that is stale or unreachable is not a revocation: the complete gets 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible with reasons: [attestation_status_stale] and resolution: recoverable, and the mandate is not paused (Conformance attestation). A debit under an open Payment Mandate on a pilot handler is not authorised by the mandate if, when the gateway sent it, the bound attestation was revoked or expired, its entry was not on the list, or the gateway was not rail_binding.conformance.sub. A verifier reports such a debit as outside the mandate from the InstructionLog payment entry of the debit, the list version and the status list. A Mandate Manager that finds the bound attestation revoked or expired tells the principal on the Trusted Surface and offers revocation.
    • A binding pause gates new complete only. It never blocks a status check, a reversal, or a refund to the original source of a debit whose rrn or utr is already in the ledger; these MUST proceed. A refund to any other destination MUST be refused (credits_to_other_party: 0). Cancel, return and issue on orders already placed under the mandate stay allowed with presence: HP (Post-order gate).
    • Pause reasons form a set. A paused mandate carries the set of reasons for which it is paused: principal_paused (a pause by the principal on the Trusted Surface), binding_ineligible, reissue_required (below), unmatched_rail_debit (a debit on a Reserve Pay block that matches no BACP payment) and block_modified (a reported increase of a block that breaks the sum of blocks or the root's headroom), the last two in the Usage ledger. The mandate stays PAUSED while any reason remains. A principal pause that arrives while a binding pause is in force is added to the set, never dropped, and a binding pause that arrives while the principal has paused the mandate is added in the same way. Only the principal, on the Trusted Surface, removes principal_paused, unmatched_rail_debit and block_modified. The gateway removes only binding_ineligible, and only on a registry_serial strictly greater than the one recorded for the pause, at which the binding is eligible again (a later publication of the same registry_version, such as a backport, has a greater serial and lifts the pause) or, for a pause caused by the gateway's conformance attestation, a newer attestation from the same authority (iss equal to rail_binding.conformance.iss), whose sub is the gateway itself, that lists the bound handler and passes every check in At complete; an attestation from another authority does not lift the pause, and the principal's Mandate Manager issues a new mandate instead, under every issuance rule. A replica that still holds the recorded or a lower serial MUST NOT remove it. When the last reason is removed the mandate returns to ACTIVE and the gateway sends mandate.status_changed (ACTIVE) to the agent; a change to the set of a mandate that stays PAUSED is sent as mandate.status_changed (PAUSED) with the new pause_reasons. While a binding reason is in the set, complete gets 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible at step 1 of evaluation; when only principal_paused, unmatched_rail_debit or block_modified remain, it gets 403 mandate_paused.
    • The gateway tells the Mandate Manager. Every use of the mandate, every relinquishment at the gateway and every addition or removal of a gateway-originated pause reason (binding_ineligible, reissue_required) is reported as a MandateEvent to the mandate's audit_uri, so that the Trusted Surface can show the principal why the agent cannot pay and can offer re-issue (Q-61, settled). The Mandate Manager derives two causes itself: a change of registry status (it reads the same registry) and a pause that follows its own issuance record. It learns the others only from the event: an attestation swap (a binding pause caused by the gateway's own attestation) and unmatched_rail_debit, which also needs a block-debit feed (Q-10). For a pilot binding the MM MUST read the attestation's status list itself and does not rely on the gateway's report. The gateway MUST also record every addition and removal of binding_ineligible or reissue_required as an InstructionLog entry at stage mandate (actor gateway) whose content carries the reason, the registry_version, the registry_serial, the registry_digest of the registry file checked and, for a lapsed attestation, its jti, in the chain of every session the change cancels, and otherwise in the mandate entry of the next session opened under the mandate (Evidence).
    • Reasons are ordered. When several apply, reasons[] of a rail_binding_not_open_eligible error lists them with reissue_required first, then binding_ineligible, then registry_copy_stale, then attestation_status_stale, and the resolution is that of the first.
    • The agent cross-checks the claim. The agent checks reissue_required and binding_ineligible against the mandate that it holds. A mandate that carries rail_binding.primitive and rail_binding.registry_version is not one that needs a re-issue, so a gateway's reissue_required for it is a claim that the agent can disprove. A claim that the agent can disprove is a gateway fault: the agent sends no POST /mandates, records gateway_fault as the reason in the mandate entry of its InstructionLog and tells the principal that the gateway refused the payment. The same holds for every other non-recoverable rail_binding_not_open_eligible: the agent never turns it into a re-issue or resume request without a new instruction from the principal.
    • The agent does not turn a binding pause into a request. This binding pause is not a principal pause: the principal does not resume it. On binding_ineligible the agent MUST NOT open a Trusted-Surface resume request or a re-issue request (POST /mandates) unless the principal gives a new instruction. It MAY tell the principal that payments under the mandate are paused and offer another payment method or an HP checkout (Revocation, pause and expiry).
    • Mandates issued before these rules are re-issued. An open Payment Mandate issued without rail_binding.primitive or rail_binding.registry_version cannot be amended, because the principal and MM signatures cover its content. The gateway MUST pause it with the reason reissue_required, and every complete under it gets 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible with reasons: [reissue_required] (not mandate_schema_invalid), so that the agent is told to have it re-issued. The gateway never removes reissue_required; the mandate MUST be re-issued through Issuance with an eligible primitive and registry_version.
    • BACP constraints MUST be at or below the descriptor's rail limits. The MM rejects a mandate that exceeds its binding. The rail is authoritative for its own limits, but MUST NOT be relied on to enforce BACP budgets (Usage ledger).
    • A Reserve Pay block is payable to one seller. Its open Payment Mandate MUST name the block's payee seller in payee{vpa, bpp_id}, and payee.vpa MUST equal the VPA in that seller's BPP-signed payment terms. A block whose payee is the buyer app (a BAP-collected block) MUST NOT back an HNP payment: the MM refuses it, and the gateway refuses such a binding, with 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible. So is a block whose seller's BPP-signed payment terms the gateway does not hold and cannot obtain (Sibling Payment Mandates).

    Per-leg payment rule for a multi-seller basket¶

    In a multi-seller basket each seller is one leg with its own session, closed mandates and debit. No payment ever spans sellers. For each leg the gateway plans the payment in this order, and takes the first that is eligible for that seller at that presence (handler intersection):

    1. The principal's preference. The handler the principal designated, ranked by rule 1 of the handler intersection. No other rung ranks above it.
    2. The seller's existing payee-bound instrument (3a). An ACTIVE sibling open Payment Mandate under the basket's root whose payee is this seller, for example a Reserve Pay block the principal already holds for it. A block backs a leg only if the leg's fresh BPP-signed on_init names the VPA that equals payee.vpa; otherwise the rung is skipped with comparison[].reason: payee_terms_changed. Held terms are usable only until the expires of their Beckn Authorization. A terms-only init that obtains terms carries no personal data of the principal beyond what the network requires (whether the network lets billing details be omitted is open with ONDC).
    3. A gift PPI the principal designated. An ACTIVE sibling open Payment Mandate bound to in.ppi.gift under the root.
    4. One planned human-present step. If any leg is left without an HNP handler, the gateway raises one step-up (confirm, reason rail_fallback) for the whole basket before any debit (Step-up). Each human-present leg is then paid with its own closed mandates and its own per-payee factor.

    Rules on the order:

    • Any-merchant primitives that are pilot (in.upi.circle, in.card.agentic) back no open mandate until a listed conformance authority attests the gateway for them (in.upi.circle also waits for Q-77), and a rung never ranks above the principal's preference. A seller that lacks only a block keeps its place and is offered the human-present step; it is not dropped from the basket.
    • The gateway MUST record, for every leg, the rung chosen and every rung skipped with its reason, as comparison[] entries in the InstructionLog (handler intersection).
    • A new block in a checkout (3b). A new per-seller block MAY be offered during a checkout only beside a one-time human-present payment to the same payee, with equal weight (the same number of steps, neither preselected; TS-2 in Human participants). It MUST NOT be offered after a failed payment, and MUST NOT be offered on a step-up whose delivery is ivr_callback. A block set up outside a checkout is exempt (Sibling Payment Mandates).
    • Fallback is a step-up. When a leg cannot be paid with the handler first planned for it (the principal's preference, or the rung the principal last approved), and the replacement changes the leg's funding class, fee-bearer, payee or collector, the gateway raises a rail_fallback step-up and moves no money. The funding class is read from the attested funding and is one of own_deposit (deposit-funded: debit, and cbdc), prepaid (prepaid and voucher) and borrowed (credit, loan and trade_credit). Prepaid is a class of its own because the principal's recourse differs: a failed on-us PPI payment is reversed by T+1 and a failed UPI merchant payment by T+5 (RBI/2019-20/67, Annex rows 8(b) and 4(b)), a refund goes back to the PPI (PPI Master Direction para 14.3(a)), and the complaint goes to the issuer within 48 hours to 30 days (para 16.2(e)). The payee is compared by the VPA and by the pair (bpp_id, provider_id), and the collector stays in the trigger, because a different payee or collector changes who the principal pays and who must refund, which no budget or rail cap shows. A change in a charge shown to the payer is already covered by the fee-bearer. A change of rail alone, with none of those changing, is logged in comparison[] with reason: rail_only_change and raises no step-up.
    • No re-route of an in-doubt leg. A leg whose payment is in_doubt MUST NOT be re-routed to another handler, and no new debit is attempted for it, until reconciliation resolves it (Failed payments).

    Settled

    The per-leg rule settles design choice (d) in 2026-10-18. Whether an agent-initiated draw on a Reserve Pay block counts as a customer action on the merchant's platform is open (Q-65).

    Fee-bearer change¶

    The payer-borne lines that apply to a handler are the session's totals[] lines of type convenience_fee or cod_fee whose applies_to_handlers lists the handler or is absent, and every line of the session's operator_charges[]. A replacement changes the leg's fee-bearer when the payer-borne lines that apply to the replacement handler differ from those that apply to the planned handler in code (the line type), amount or recipient. A line with no handler scope applies to every handler, so on its own it never changes the fee-bearer. The merchant discount rate, the payment aggregator's commission, the finder fee and every other cost the seller bears are never in the comparison. The trigger therefore fires mainly on a fallback to or from COD, on a change of operator fee and on a quote that charges one debit mode more than another.

    Step-up¶

    A step-up hands the decision back to the human. It is never only an error: the session moves to requires_escalation, carries a step_up object, and its messages[] includes step_up_required with resolution: requires_buyer_review. The exception is a post_order step-up, which has no session: it comes in Error.step_up of the 403 presence_not_proven that asks for it, and its approval yields the hp_proof for one write (Presence).

    There are three kinds.

    kind Meaning Triggers (reason)
    confirm The principal confirms this checkout on the Trusted Surface. above_step_up_threshold: amount strictly above step_up.above_amount, or a suspected split order. new_merchant: step_up.new_merchant is set and the seller has not been used under this mandate. outside_categories: step_up.outside_categories is set and a category is outside categories_allow. first_order: step_up.first_order is set and this is the first order under the mandate. constraint_unresolved: missing disclosures, drip pricing, a price that rose above the discovered price of an item shown with a prior_price (discovery_price_changed), a convenience fee that the mandate's convenience_fees does not cover in HNP, an operator charge that the mandate's operator_compensation does not cover, the HNP payment_mode_fee_inconsistent warning (Fees on the payer's screen), an order that a seller's requires_principal_confirmation covers, or any check the gateway cannot resolve. seller_confirmation: a BPP-signed on_select or on_init carries the bacp tag seller_confirmation, so the seller's request for the buyer's own confirmation reaches the principal as a template sentence (Seller refusals and substitution); at most one per checkout_hash. rail_fallback: a leg of a multi-seller basket has no HNP handler, or its replacement payment changes the funding class, fee-bearer, payee or collector (Per-leg rule).
    rail_auth The rail requires a fresh per-transaction factor. above_afa_limit: amount strictly above ₹15,000 (₹1 lakh for the categories the rail lists). afa_threshold_card_emandate, afa_crossborder_cnp, split_tender_afa_on_total.
    post_order The principal confirms one post-order write or trade act, to obtain hp_proof. It is carried in Error.step_up of 403 presence_not_proven, not in a session. At most one is pending per (order_id, action, issue_id), and a repeat of the refused write returns it; creation is rate-limited per (agent, principal) and per order (429 with Retry-After); a replacement for a step-up voided after a counterparty revised the issue's resolution is not counted there and is capped per (principal, issue_id); and a repeat after an explicit decline waits out the published cool-off (409) (Limits on step-ups). presence_proof: a write with presence: HP and no valid hp_proof (Presence).

    Any other rail cap (per-transaction, per-period, remaining block, first 24 hours, channel caps) does not escalate. It returns 403 rail_limit_exceeded with resolution: requires_buyer_review, step_up_kind: rail_auth, and reasons[] listing every local and rail reason.

    Flow.

    1. The gateway creates a step_up with a step_up_id, a delivery (deep_link, app_push, ivr_callback or assisted), a trusted_surface_url and a surface (not with app_push, which carries no link) and an expires_at at most 15 minutes ahead, and at least 5 minutes ahead for ivr_callback and assisted (a BACP design floor, not an external requirement), and sends session.updated. ivr_callback is Proposed (Q-63). The gateway mints trusted_surface_url only on an origin of the step-up's signer (surface is the signer's subscriber id). If no origin of the signer conforms, it uses app_push through the signer's ts_notify_url, a content-free push, and otherwise answers 503 trusted_surface_unavailable (Origins and delivery).
    2. Render binding. The gateway signs a ts_render for the step-up, once, carrying its checkout_hash, the single-figure total and the breakup hash, and never changes it. The Trusted Surface fetches it by step_up_id (GET /trusted-surface/step-ups/{step_up_id}, tag bacp-ts) with the key of the step-up's Mandate Manager or payer PSP: for a session step-up, the issuer of the session's mandate or the PSP in rail_binding.psp_id of the leg's planned open Payment Mandate (the one under the session's root that the gateway planned to pay the session with); for a summary step-up over a basket, the root's Mandate Manager only; for a post_order step-up, the issuer of the order's mandate or the PSP in auth_binding.psp_id of the order's closed Payment Mandate(s) (Step-up signers); any other registry key gets 404 not_found. It renders only from the ts_render and its own templates; the trusted_surface_url that the agent delivers carries only a reference, never content.
    3. The principal approves or declines on the Trusted Surface. The MM or PSP posts step_up.approved or step_up.declined to /trusted-surface/events. The agent cannot post these events (403 trusted_surface_only), and a Mandate Manager or PSP other than the step-up's gets 404 not_found. step_up.approved MUST carry a Presentation whose content_hash is the sha-256 of the ts_render and whose checkout_hash is REQUIRED and is the quote's (for a post_order step-up, the checkout_hash of the order's closed Checkout Mandate), except on the approval of a post_order step-up for an order-less ledger act (LEDGER_ACK with no order_id), which carries none; its user_action is never decline; its device_signature, where present, is a signature by a device key of the Trusted Surface over the JCS form of {step_up_id, content_hash, acted_at}, which MUST verify against principal.device_jkt[] where the mandate lists it. The gateway refuses an approval without presentation, without presentation.checkout_hash (where it is required) or with user_action: decline with 400 invalid_request, one whose content_hash does not match with 403 ts_render_mismatch, and one acted on after expires_at as expired, each without changing the session. While the session is in human_handoff, an approval is held, not applied, until the handoff is released (Human handoff).
    4. On a confirm-only approval the session returns to ready_for_complete with message step_up_approved. The agent calls complete again with step_up_id, still under the open mandate and its budgets.
    5. On an HP payment approval the event carries principal-signed closed mandates (auth_binding.type: upi.pin) and the PSP-signed receipt. The gateway verifies them and confirms the order without drawing on the open mandate's binding.
    6. On decline, or once expires_at passes, the session becomes cancelled with step_up_declined or step_up_expired, and no money moves.

    Summary step-up over a basket. For a multi-seller basket the gateway raises one rail_fallback step-up for the whole plan, before any debit. It belongs to the session whose complete raised it (its ts_render.session_id and checkout_hash are that session's), and the same step_up is shown on every session of the basket. Its ts_render carries basket[], one line per seller (Render input), and its step_up and ts_render carry covers[]: one entry {session_id, checkout_hash, payee, amount} per leg the approval covers.

    • A summary approves a plan, never a payment. It never replaces a per-payee factor: each human-present leg still needs its own closed mandates signed on the Trusted Surface and its own factor, and an HNP leg still draws only on its own payee-bound instrument.
    • The complete of a covered session skips step 8 of evaluation only by consuming its covers[] entry. The entry is consumed once, inside the atomic section keyed on the shared root open Checkout Mandate (Usage ledger), and only while the session's current checkout_hash, payee and amount equal the entry. An entry already used, a session re-quoted since the approval, or a session that no entry names gets requires_escalation and moves no money.
    • Only the shared root's Mandate Manager fetches the summary ts_render and posts its result (Step-up signers). No payer PSP does, so that no PSP learns the rest of the basket; any other key, including a payer PSP of an open Payment Mandate under the root, gets 404 not_found. While a session is in human_handoff, the approval is held as for any step-up, and on release it applies to that session only if its covers[] entry still equals the session's checkout_hash, payee and amount.
    • When the summary is declined or expires, every covers[] entry in it is void, for every covered leg: a covered session's complete then gets requires_escalation as for a session that no entry names, and no money moves on the summary.

    Split-order detection. To stop an agent splitting one purchase into several orders that each stay under step_up.above_amount, the gateway MUST escalate (confirm, above_step_up_threshold, with message split_order_suspected) any order where the other orders under the same root open Checkout Mandate to the same seller, completed or in flight within the previous 10 minutes, plus this order, exceed above_amount. The seller is identified by the tax id (the GSTIN) in the BPP-signed terms, else by the payee VPA, else by the pair bpp_id and provider_id, so that one shop that signs on through two seller apps is one seller. Two orders are the same seller when any of these identifiers match. The covers[] legs of a summary-approved basket are exempt, because the principal has approved each of them. The check runs inside the atomic section (Usage ledger) and before rail caps. Velocity across different sellers is bounded only by the budgets (Q-50, settled).

    Deterministic evaluation¶

    The gateway evaluates mandates in non-LLM code. It runs the evaluation as an advisory pre-check on PUT /sessions/{id} and authoritatively on POST /sessions/{id}/complete. Evaluation is deterministic and fails closed. The first failing step decides the result, and any exception or unexpected input is a failure (403 mandate_invalid), never a pass.

    Before evaluation, the transport layer has already verified the request signature, the AgentCredential and its status, the signature tag (bacp-pay for complete), the replay cache and the idempotency key (Identity and Request Signing).

    1. Positive amount. The payable amount MUST be an integer number of paise greater than zero; a boolean is not an integer. Otherwise 403 amount_not_positive (unrecoverable), before any other step and before any reservation.
    2. Structure. Unknown constraint keys give 403 constraint_unknown. A schema failure gives 400 mandate_schema_invalid. A rail binding that is not eligible against the current registry and the gateway's conformance attestation, or an open Payment Mandate issued without rail_binding.primitive or rail_binding.registry_version, gives 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible (Handler binding). For an open Payment Mandate whose only schema failure is a missing rail_binding.primitive or rail_binding.registry_version, rail_binding_not_open_eligible takes precedence over mandate_schema_invalid.
    3. Open mandate. The principal and MM signatures verify (403 mandate_invalid_signature). The rail attestation verifies and matches the binding. The status list does not mark the mandate revoked (403 mandate_revoked). The mandate belongs to this gateway: agent.bap_id equals the subscriber id of this gateway's BAP (403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.agent.bap_id), or, for a mandate issued without agent.bap_id, the hash of the presented AgentCredential equals agent.credential_hash (the same error at $.agent.credential_hash). status is ACTIVE (otherwise mandate_revoked, mandate_paused, mandate_exhausted, mandate_expired, or mandate_required for PENDING and REJECTED). The current time is within valid_from to valid_until (mandate_expired). The action is in action_scope (mandate_scope_mismatch).
    4. Presence. presence is in presence_allowed (403 presence_not_allowed).
    5. Closed mandates. They are signed by the parent's cnf key (HNP) or principal (HP) (mandate_invalid_signature). parent_mandate_id binds. Validity is narrowed and current. checkout_hash equals the hash of the BPP-signed quote. amount equals the quote total or, on a closed Payment Mandate that carries tender{plan_hash, tender_id}, the amount of that tender of the session's payment plan (Wire binding of plans). The payee matches. checkout_mandate_ref links the pair. An HNP auth_binding.type is parent_rail_binding. The closed Payment Mandate has not been used, and no reservation or settled debit exists for this checkout_hash under the root open Checkout Mandate (for each tender under a payment plan). The gateway makes the check inside the root's atomic section, so that of two parallel complete calls one wins. Any mismatch gives 403 mandate_scope_mismatch.
    6. Constraints. In order: categories_deny, categories_allow, merchants_allow, delivery_pincodes, line_items, amount_per_txn (mandate_scope_mismatch); then each budgets[] entry and max_executions against exposure (mandate_exhausted).
    7. Split-order detection, as in Step-up: a confirm step-up.
    8. Rail caps. AFA thresholds give a rail_auth step-up. Every other local or rail cap (attested rail_limits, remaining block, period and first-24-hour caps, binding expiry, and the PSP's own pre-check) gives 403 rail_limit_exceeded with all reasons.
    9. Step-up thresholds. above_amount, new_merchant, outside_categories and first_order give a confirm step-up. This step is skipped only while the session's current checkout_hash equals the checkout_hash of the approved step-up's Presentation and its current disclosures.hash equals the price_breakup_hash of the approved render. Once the quote changes, the approval no longer applies: step 8 runs again and a new step-up is needed, and no money moves on the old approval. A step_up_id that belongs to another session is 404 not_found, unless the step-up is an approved summary step-up whose covers[] names this session, in which case the step is skipped by consuming that entry (Step-up).
    10. Disclosures and dark patterns. Mandatory disclosures are present and hashed, pre-ticked add-ons are removed and drip pricing is flagged (see Sessions and Checkout).
    11. Data. Shared fields are within data_scopes and covered by an active ConsentReceipt (Consent).
    12. Evidence. The session's InstructionLog chain verifies and its head matches (403 log_chain_invalid; see Evidence).
    13. Collector. On complete, the party in collected_by is an RBI-authorised payment aggregator or a bank, according to a verified rbi_pa or bank entry in the regulatory_status of its registry record (403 collector_not_authorised). A declared entry, or none, fails.
    14. Commit. Consume the closed Payment Mandate (one use) and any covers[] entry used at step 8, create the budget reservation, move the session to complete_in_progress, increment usage.count and report the use to audit_uri as a MandateEvent of type use.

    Every amount in steps 0, 4, 5, 7 and 8 is the BPP-signed quote total after discounts (Discounts).

    Steps 9 and 10 are checked when the session is updated and re-checked before complete. A gateway MAY run steps 9 to 12 before it enters the atomic section, since they do not depend on the ledger. Steps 5 to 8 and 13, and the checkout_hash check of step 4, MUST run inside it.

    Usage ledger, reservations and concurrency¶

    The gateway's mandate ledger is the source of truth for usage. The usage object on a mandate is a read-only view of it.

    • Settled spend comes only from PSP-signed receipts. A budget decrements only when the gateway applies a successful payment Receipt whose signature verifies against a registry key of role PSP and whose open_mandate_ref (or mandate_ref) is this mandate. Agent claims never change usage. A payment success or failure receipt applies only when it is signed by the PSP of the closed Payment Mandate's auth_binding.psp_id (for an HNP payment, the rail_binding.psp_id of the open Payment Mandate), so that another PSP's key never releases or settles a reservation. A refund receipt applies only to a payment of the same checkout_hash and payee, which it names by original_rrn (Refund receipts). Each receipt_id and each rail reference is applied once. A receipt that fails these checks is refused (400 receipt_invalid) and the reservation stays.
    • Reservations. For every open Payment Mandate the ledger also holds in-flight reservations, keyed by closed Payment Mandate id, each with an amount and a time. Exposure is settled spend plus reservations. Exposure against an open Checkout Mandate's budgets is the settled spend and reservations of every open Payment Mandate under it. Budgets, max_executions, every rail-cap pre-check and split-order detection MUST count reservations as spent. A firm ACCEPT, counter-offer or bid that the negotiation guard emits holds a reservation keyed by its offer_hash. The gateway writes it into the ledger of the root open Checkout Mandate, so that exposure inside the root's atomic section counts it. A complete of the negotiated deal converts that reservation into the reservation of the closed Payment Mandate, in one step inside the same section, and never counts both.
    • Atomic section. For complete, steps 5 to 8 and 13, and the checkout_hash check of step 4, of Deterministic evaluation MUST run as one atomic, linearisable operation keyed on the root open Checkout Mandate: the open Checkout Mandate named by the open Payment Mandate's checkout_mandate_ref, followed through parent_mandate_id to the top of any delegation chain. Mutual exclusion keyed on that mandate_id, or compare-and-reserve on a store with equivalent guarantees, satisfies this. Sibling open Payment Mandates under one open Checkout Mandate therefore share one atomic section, and a split-tender payment plan reserves every tender in one section. The rail debit MUST run after the atomic section, never inside it (Atomic reservation). A post-order write consumes its hp_proof in the section keyed on the order's root open Checkout Mandate, or on its closed Checkout Mandate when the order was placed with no open mandate (Presence proof).
    • Blocks are exposure. A Reserve Pay block is a pull authority: the seller may debit it several times. Each block bound by an open Payment Mandate under the root counts as reserved spend at the larger of its attested block_amount and the settled debits matched to it plus the reservations on it in every budget window it overlaps, from activation until its revocation at the rail is confirmed (rail_revocation.status: confirmed) or it expires, and after its root ends it counts against any new root of the same principal (Sibling Payment Mandates). A block that a mandate.rejected or mandate.revoked event reports with rail_revocation.status: unconfirmed, including one refused at step 6 or step 7 of Issuance after the rail call and so never activated, is exposure in the same way: at its attested block_amount, from the moment the gateway receives the event, under the root it was requested for (or, for a request without checkout_mandate_ref, against every root of the same principal), until the MM reports it confirmed or the block expires. Such a block never counts as ₹0. Settled debits matched to the block (PSP-signed receipts on its mandate) count within that reservation, never on top of it. The atomic section, every budget check, the headroom check on the activation of a block and the sum-of-blocks rule use this exposure.
    • Unmatched rail debits. Where the payer PSP attests that it reports every debit on the block to the gateway (a block-debit feed), a debit on the block that matches no BACP payment counts as settled spend, and the gateway adds the pause reason unmatched_rail_debit to the block's open Payment Mandate (pause reasons). Only the principal, on the Trusted Surface, removes it. The pause stops BACP payments but not draws at the rail, so while it is set the Trusted Surface also shows the principal the route to revoke the block in the UPI app (NPCI UPI Consolidated Circular v1.0, para 16.10). Where no such feed is attested, the gateway cannot see such a debit. The unseen-draw statement then says, in the principal's language, that this seller can draw up to ₹X unseen by BACP, where X is the block's attested amount less the matched debits (at issuance, the requested block amount), and that the principal can change the block's amount or validity through the seller's platform, which the payee PSP must offer (Consolidated Circular para 16.9), a change that BACP does not see. It appears in the notice of the block's issuance (Issuance, step 4) and in the ts_render of every step-up that lists the block. The form of a block-debit feed and of its attestation is not defined in this version (Q-10, whose scope includes modifications of a block).
    • Block modification. An agent MUST NOT request a modification of a block: a request from an agent gets 403 trusted_surface_only. Where the payer PSP attests a block-debit feed it MAY also report a modification as the event rail.block_modified (mandate_id, the new block_amount and, if it changed, block_expires_at), signed by the registry key of the PSP named in rail_binding.psp_id and posted to POST /trusted-surface/events. The gateway applies it inside the root's atomic section, so the block counts at the larger of the new amount and its matched debits plus reservations. An increase that breaks the sum-of-blocks rule or the root's headroom adds the pause reason block_modified; only the principal, on the Trusted Surface, removes it, by approving the modified block against a fresh PSP attestation. Where no feed is attested, a modification made on the seller's app is invisible to BACP, as the unseen-draw statement says.
    • Resolution. A reservation becomes settled spend only when a PSP-signed success receipt is applied. It is released on a PSP-signed failure receipt or a refused debit. While a payment is in_doubt its reservation is kept until reconciliation resolves it. When every payment on a mandate has resolved, no reservation remains.
    • Pre-checks never reserve. The PUT pre-check is advisory and MUST NOT create a reservation. A step-up or reject outcome MUST NOT create one either.
    • The rail is not the backstop. A gateway MUST NOT rely on rail-enforced limits to bound BACP budgets. A rail authority can be far larger than the budget.
    • Replicas. A gateway that runs as several instances MUST share the ledger and keep it linearisable per root open Checkout Mandate (a single writer or a transactional store). A per-process lock is not enough. The order of the principal-keyed section and the root's section is in Atomic reservation. Budgets are atomic per root inside one gateway, and one open mandate belongs to one gateway (agent.bap_id), so a principal with two BAPs cannot spend one mandate through both; a mandate moves between gateways only by a successor root (Porting a mandate, Q-36, settled).

    Conformance. N concurrent complete calls on one open mandate, or on sibling open Payment Mandates under one open Checkout Mandate, MUST NOT settle more than any budgets[].max allows, even when the rail authority is far larger than the budget, and MUST NOT leave reservations behind.

    Positive amounts only. Every payable amount (a closed mandate amount, a payment Receipt amount, a split-plan tender) MUST be greater than zero. Direction is never carried by sign. Amount.value keeps minimum: 0 in the schemas for values that may legitimately be zero, such as a free line or a remaining budget.

    Refunds do not restore budget, with one narrow exception. A refund, whether from a cancellation, a return or a failed confirmation, MUST NOT reduce settled spend or restore any budget or max_executions count. This is fail-safe: an agent cannot cycle purchases and returns to exceed its authority.

    The exception is a debit that bought nothing. The gateway releases the spend of a debit, and the budget headroom and max_executions count it took, when all of these hold:

    1. the debit is a debited_not_confirmed debit with no order_id, or its order was cancelled by a BPP-signed on_cancel before fulfilment;
    2. the debit is refunded in full to its source, and the refund is settled, not merely started; and
    3. the evidence is either (a) a refund receipt signed by the PSP of the closed Payment Mandate's auth_binding.psp_id (for an HNP payment, the rail_binding.psp_id) that cites original_rrn for the full amount, or (b) a refund receipt signed by the registered PSP of the order's collected_by that cites original_rrn for the full amount, and the principal's confirmation on the Trusted Surface that the credit arrived (a confirm step-up with reason refund_received, whose render says "I have received ₹X" in the principal's language, and whose approval is the principal's own factor).

    A downward weight adjustment of a weighed line (Weighed goods) comes after fulfilment and is a partial refund, so it releases nothing. An upward adjustment that an adjustment_band allows is collected from the hold placed at payment and stays inside the budget that the hold took.

    A receipt of the collector's PSP alone moves the session to reversed and leaves the spend in place until the principal confirms, so that a seller with an insider at its payment aggregator cannot restore the budget by forging a refund. A partial refund, a refund to anything but the source (for example into in.store_credit) and a refund on initiation alone release nothing. While such a reversal is unresolved, that is while the session is reversal_pending, the gateway refuses every further human-not-present debit to that seller under the root with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch and reasons: [seller_reversal_unresolved]. A human-present payment to the seller, and any payment to another seller, is unaffected, and the hold ends only when the debit's release holds under path (a) or (b) above, or when the principal lifts it on the Trusted Surface. A receipt of the collector's PSP alone moves payment.status to reversed but keeps the hold. A late on_confirm after a reversal has begun is answered with a Beckn cancel, logged, and never debited again; whether a late on_confirm forms a contract is for counsel (Q-52). Whether the hold is a ranking parameter that a gateway that is an e-commerce entity must disclose from 01-01-2027 is for counsel (Q-66).

    budget_after. After applying a receipt the gateway computes the remaining amount of each budget from its ledger and returns it, unsigned, as session.payment.budget_after and as an info message budget_after. It is never part of a signed Receipt.

    Mandate events¶

    Step 13 of evaluation already requires the gateway to report a use to the mandate's audit_uri, the endpoint of the Mandate Manager that issued it. This section gives that report its payload. It adds no endpoint to the Mandate Manager's API: the audit_uri is the one the mandate already carries (component MandateEvent and webhook mandate.event of the OpenAPI document).

    • Types. use (a closed Payment Mandate was consumed, with its amount), relinquished (the agent gave up the mandate or its root at the gateway), pause_changed (a gateway-originated pause reason was added or removed, with change, reason, registry_version, registry_serial and, for an attestation, jti) and closed (the gateway's last event for a mandate whose root was revoked, with last_seq, the seq of the last event before it; the closed event itself carries the next seq).
    • Fields. event_id, mandate_id, root_mandate_id, gateway (the BAP subscriber id), created_at (IST, +05:30), seq and prev_hash. The events of one mandate_id are numbered from 0 with no gap, and prev_hash is the sha-256 over the JCS form of the previous event of that mandate (for seq 0, over the mandate_id as UTF-8 bytes), as the InstructionLog does for a session.
    • Signature. The gateway signs the event under RFC 9421 with the registry key of its BAP, under the tag bacp-mm-event (Signature tags). The tag is never an agent tag, and an agent key never signs it. The Mandate Manager refuses an event, and records nothing, when the BAP of the signing key is not gateway or is not the mandate's agent.bap_id (for a legacy mandate without one, the BAP of the credential whose hash the mandate carries).
    • Delivery. Within 60 seconds of the change, the bound that revocation.notify_sla_seconds already sets. A failed delivery is retried with the same event_id, and the Mandate Manager de-duplicates on it. A delivery failure MUST NOT block a refund, a reversal, a status check or a post-order write, and no payment depends on an event.
    • Gaps. When the Mandate Manager receives a seq after a missing one, or holds fewer events than a closed event's last_seq names, it records event_gap for the mandate and the Trusted Surface shows the mandate as "unreconciled". An unreconciled mandate loses only the explanation of why payments paused. A window of spend that the Mandate Manager cannot reconstruct from its events is incomplete.
    • Log. The Mandate Manager's event store is the mandate-scoped log, kept for the mandate's retention period. The gateway keeps writing the addition and removal of a pause reason to the InstructionLog of the session that it cancels, or to the mandate entry of the next session under the mandate, and adds a reference to the MandateEvent to it.

    Revocation, pause and expiry¶

    stateDiagram-v2

    PENDING --> ACTIVE: principal approves on Trusted Surface

    • Principals revoke, pause and resume only on the Trusted Surface, or with the rail's own controls such as a UPI safety switch. The agent can give up its own authority with POST /mandates/{id}/relinquish, which makes the mandate REVOKED.
    • On revocation the MM marks the mandate in its status list and sends a signed mandate.revoked (or rail.safety_switch) event to the gateway. revocation.notify_sla_seconds MUST be 60 or less, and the gateway MUST notify the agent (webhook mandate.status_changed, with affected_sessions) within that time.
    • The gateway MUST then cancel every open session under the mandate (incomplete, ready_for_complete, requires_escalation, human_handoff) with message mandate_revoked, and refuse every new commitment: complete returns 403 mandate_revoked. A pause by the principal and expiry behave the same way with mandate_paused and mandate_expired. A principal pause adds the reason principal_paused, and a principal resume removes only that reason (pause reasons).
    • Revocation, every pause (whatever its reason), expiry and exhaustion stop new commitments only. Cancel, return and issue on orders already placed under the mandate stay allowed as protective actions, with presence: HP (Post-order gate).
    • If a debit races a revocation, the rail rejects it, because the Trusted Surface revokes the rail primitive first.
    • Ending a root ends its siblings. When a root open Checkout Mandate ends in any way (revoked by the principal, by a rail safety switch or by the agent's relinquishment, or EXPIRED), every open Payment Mandate under it is revoked too. The Mandate Manager MUST revoke each payee-bound primitive (each Reserve Pay block) at the rail, one by one, and MUST send one signed mandate.revoked event per sibling open Payment Mandate, carrying cascade_of (the root's mandate_id) and rail_revocation{status} (confirmed or unconfirmed) for its block. The gateway reports each to the agent in mandate.status_changed with the same two fields. A block whose revocation is unconfirmed stays exposure in the ledger, including against any new root of the same principal (Usage ledger), until the MM reports it confirmed or the block expires. The Trusted Surface, on the revocation and in any later ts_render that lists the block, shows the principal the route to revoke it in the UPI app, where the payee PSP and the UPI app must provide a revoke facility (NPCI UPI Consolidated Circular v1.0, para 16.10). The gateway treats every sibling as revoked as soon as it learns that the root has ended, without waiting for the MM's events. A relinquishment at the gateway reaches the MM as a MandateEvent of type relinquished, sent within 60 seconds (Mandate events). A relinquishment of a root that has blocks is not complete for the principal until the MM reports rail_revocation for each of them. Until then the agent MUST give the principal the Trusted Surface revoke route for the block, and the MM answers the relinquishment with mandate.revoked events that carry cascade_of and rail_revocation{status}. The gateway sends mandate.status_changed to the agent for a gateway-originated change within the same 60 seconds. No BACP route to revoke needs more steps or factors than the rail's own withdrawal (NPCI UPI Consolidated Circular v1.0, paras 14.23 and 16.10).
    • A binding pause is different. The gateway adds the reason binding_ineligible when a mandate's rail binding stops being eligible after a registry or conformance-attestation change, and reissue_required to an open Payment Mandate issued without rail_binding.primitive or rail_binding.registry_version. Sessions are cancelled with rail_binding_not_open_eligible, and while a binding reason is in the set, complete returns 400 rail_binding_not_open_eligible, not mandate_paused. The gateway removes binding_ineligible itself on a strictly greater registry_serial in which the binding is eligible, or, for a pause caused by its conformance attestation, on a newer attestation from the same authority that lists the pilot and passes every check in At complete, never principal_paused or reissue_required, and the mandate stays PAUSED while any reason remains. The gateway reports the pause and the lift to the MM as MandateEvents of type pause_changed (Mandate events). Pause and lift are written to the InstructionLog with the registry_version and registry_serial (Handler binding).
    • A mandate becomes EXPIRED after valid_until, or when its rail binding expires (rail_limits.expires_at). Validity is bounded by the purpose's maximum validity.

    DPDP consent and data minimisation¶

    ConsentReceipt¶

    Every merchant that receives personal data is a separate Data Fiduciary. The MM issues one ConsentReceipt per recipient per mandate, signed with its registry key. The MM acts as a DPDP Consent Manager only if it is registered as one (see below). The receipt records:

    • the itemised data_items (field, and form: plain, masked, selective_disclosure_vc or token);
    • the purpose and the goods or services;
    • the recipient (fiduciary_name, bpp_id, provider_id, dpo_contact);
    • the notice (language, hash, uri, and translation_model for translated notices);
    • captured_via: the Trusted Surface (never the agent), the principal's affirmative action and a device signature; a voice capture adds audio and transcript hashes and the translation model;
    • the withdrawal route (as easy as giving consent; erasure_signal: true) and the grievance route;
    • retention: recipient_max and legal_minimum (default P1Y, DPDP Rule 8(3));
    • the legal_basis: legitimate_use_s7a only for delivery data the principal voluntarily provided for that order, and consent_s6 for everything else.

    In HNP shopping the seller is often unknown when the mandate is set up. The MM then issues a class receipt with recipient.bpp_id: "*", scoped to the mandate's categories. A class receipt only pre-authorises. The MM MUST mint a per-seller ConsentReceipt, whose recipient.bpp_id is that seller, at init and before any personal data reaches the seller; no data is ever released on the strength of a class receipt alone (settled in Q-12).

    Fail-safe default until Q-12 is settled

    Whether a class pre-authorisation is valid consent under DPDP Act ss.5–6 and Rule 3 is open (Q-12). Until it is settled, and from 13-05-2027, when those provisions apply, the first release of personal data to a seller not named in any receipt the principal approved MUST be confirmed by the principal on the Trusted Surface, which shows that seller's notice. The gateway escalates with resolution requires_buyer_review and releases nothing before the confirmation.

    Marketing consent¶

    Marketing is not part of a purchase, a mandate or a spend, so it has its own consent event. It is never a purpose.code of a mandate, and a ConsentReceipt for personal data (the receipt above) never covers it.

    Fail-safe (normative). With no active marketing ConsentReceipt for a seller, the gateway and the Mandate Manager report every marketing purpose of that seller as granted: false. A seller's own default, a UCP buyer.consent member (source: "business" is a default, not consent), a class receipt (recipient.bpp_id: "*") and a receipt for personal data never grant one. The principal is never asked for marketing consent inside a payment step-up: a Presentation of a bacp-pay step-up that carries a marketing grant is refused, because consent is its own event on the Trusted Surface, one purpose and one channel at a time, with "no" as the first or an equal option.

    Proposed

    The receipt below is Proposed: it can change shape in a later dated release without a !, because a Proposed rule is not yet part of what a conforming implementation must do. Whether it satisfies DPDP s.6(1) and the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018 together is a question for counsel (Q-101); the fail-safe above does not depend on the answer.

    A marketing ConsentReceipt has consent_kind: "marketing" (consent-receipt.json). The Mandate Manager issues it, and the principal gives it on the Trusted Surface (captured_via, never the agent). It has no mandate_ref, no data_items and no purpose. Instead it carries purposes[] (each {code, text, granted}, none preselected) and channels[] (sms, whatsapp, voice_call, email, push), and it reuses notice, captured_via, withdrawal (as easy as giving consent), grievance and retention. recipient.bpp_id MUST NOT be "*": a marketing receipt names one seller. A purpose that the principal was not asked about does not appear and is not granted.

    Consent Manager registration¶

    DPDP Rule 4 and Act s.6(9) apply from 13-11-2026. How the MM relates to a registered Consent Manager is open (Q-16). Until it is settled, these fail-safe rules apply:

    • An MM MUST NOT act, or describe itself, as a Consent Manager unless it is registered with the Data Protection Board.
    • On receipts issued on or after 13-11-2026, cm_registration_no MUST be the Board registration number when the MM acts as a Consent Manager, and MUST be null otherwise. A null value means the receipt records consent given to the recipient Data Fiduciary, not through a Consent Manager.
    • An MM that acts as a registered Consent Manager MUST NOT also act as credential provider (re-verifying closed mandates, issuing within_bounds attestations); that role runs as a logically separate component with its own registry key.

    Data minimisation¶

    • principal.ref is a pairwise pseudonymous id at the MM. It is never an Aadhaar number, and no BACP artefact carries one.
    • principal.device_jkt[] is optional: the RFC 7638 thumbprints of the device keys that the principal enrolled on the Trusted Surface at issuance. It is not a constraint, so a gateway that does not know it ignores it. Where it is present, a Presentation's device_signature MUST verify against one of the keys (Presentation record); its absence never weakens an approval, so a feature-phone principal keeps an equal path.
    • data_scopes lists the only personal data fields the agent may release: NAME, PHONE, DELIVERY_ADDRESS, EMAIL, AGE_OVER_18, GSTIN, ACTING_FOR. ACTING_FOR allows the gateway to tell the seller the GSTIN and display name of whom the agent acts for (context.bacp.acting_for.gstin and display_name, and their mirror tags); without it they are omitted. The purpose limits which scopes may be requested. The gateway sends acting_for to a seller only under a per-seller ConsentReceipt whose data_items[] lists ACTING_FOR, so that the principal's consent to this disclosure is on record for that seller. For a sole proprietor, whose GSTIN or name is personal data, this serves the DPDP Act, 2023, s.6(1) and s.6(10) from 13-05-2027. A request carries no buyer GSTIN of its own: the only GSTIN that reaches a seller is acting_for.gstin, which the mandate's data_scopes fix, and a request that names a GSTIN other than that one, or any GSTIN under a mandate without ACTING_FOR, is refused with 403 mandate_scope_mismatch before anything is sent to the seller. On an ONDC B2B order the gateway projects acting_for.gstin into the order's buyer_id.
    • When a session update shares data (data_share), the gateway MUST check that SHARE_DATA is in action_scope, that every field is in data_scopes, and that consent_receipt_ref names an active, not withdrawn, ConsentReceipt of this mandate (the class receipt, or a per-seller receipt for the session's seller). Before any personal data is sent to the seller at init, the gateway MUST hold an active per-seller ConsentReceipt from the MM whose recipient.bpp_id is that seller. Otherwise it returns 403 mandate_scope_mismatch at $.data_share.fields or $.data_share.consent_receipt_ref, before anything is sent to the seller.
    • No personal data travels through the Beckn gateway in search. Delivery data goes only to the chosen seller, at init.
    • The payment leg is basket-blind: the PSP receives payee, amount, mandate reference and checkout_hash only.
    • Webhooks MUST NOT carry personal data outside the receiving agent's data_scopes.
    • The MM is data-blind: it holds mandates, notices, consent records and usage, never order or payment payloads.
    • When the principal withdraws consent on the Trusted Surface (consent.withdrawn), the receipt becomes WITHDRAWN and further data release under it is refused.

    The MM keeps mandate, notice, consent and sharing records for at least 7 years in machine-readable form, and MUST give them to the principal on request, on the Trusted Surface, in machine-readable form. A REST export interface is not part of this version (Q-16), and the full retention schedule is open (Q-17). See also Evidence: retention and Security and Privacy.
